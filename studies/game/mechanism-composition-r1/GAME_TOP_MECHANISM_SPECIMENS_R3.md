## New motifs

### m24 — Knowledge can be the only permanent upgrade

A game can reset world/inventory state while preserving player knowledge, making understanding the primary progression resource.

**Seen in:** top-outer-wilds

### m25 — Information architecture can manufacture cooperation

Splitting task-relevant information across roles can make communication itself a mechanic rather than a support channel.

**Seen in:** top-keep-talking

### m26 — Boss mastery works on two parallel channels

Pattern learning and equipment/build preparation can coexist as separate ways to convert repeated failure into future advantage.

**Seen in:** top-monster-hunter, top-elden-ring

### m27 — Difficulty can be a configurable contract

Assist/difficulty controls can tune timing, complexity or procedure while preserving a recognizable core grammar.

**Seen in:** top-celeste, top-msfs2024

### m28 — Pause can be a cognition mechanic

Real-time-with-pause can preserve crisis pressure while giving explicit time to inspect state, allocate resources and issue commands.

**Seen in:** top-ftl

### m29 — Rule growth can revalue routine work

A repetitive verb can remain deep when the governing rules change and speed, accuracy and morality share one constrained workflow.

**Seen in:** top-papers-please

### m30 — Indirect control needs delayed causality to remain legible

Management play works when policy, people and resource choices produce delayed simulated consequences that can still be explained well enough for model revision.

**Seen in:** top-football-manager, top-rimworld

### m31 — High-fidelity simulation reuses one grammar across many contexts

Stable continuous rules can support long-term mastery when environment/scenario changes the problem without replacing the control model.

**Seen in:** top-msfs2024, top-rocket-league

### m32 — Failure can produce content instead of reset

Failure becomes less punitive when it generates new narrative/state information rather than merely denying progress or forcing repetition.

**Seen in:** top-disco-elysium, top-hades

### m33 — Choice itself can become a mechanic under interpretation

Choice gains another layer when the system or characters explicitly interpret the player act, not only branch the next content node.

**Seen in:** top-stanley-parable, top-bg3
