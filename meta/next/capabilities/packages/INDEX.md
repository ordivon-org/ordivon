## Shared disciplines available to every package

Knowledge Management, Decision Science/DSS, Operations Research, Systems Engineering, BPM/workflow/case management, V&V/quality, risk management, governance, human factors, scientific method and statistics are external mature disciplines. They are selected when useful; Ordivon does not reimplement them as common internal services.

## Execution universe

Agents, Skills, programs, solvers, databases, workflow engines, APIs, humans and infrastructure are enabling capabilities. A tool being important does not make it a Package.
