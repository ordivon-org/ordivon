## Why no merge

The three lenses answer different questions:

~~~text
unknown object or concept
  -> C-K Design

known comparable repeated choices with attributable feedback
  -> Exploration Policy

known representation plus trustworthy evaluator plus population variation
  -> Evolutionary Search
~~~

They should not be activated together merely because a task contains the word search.
