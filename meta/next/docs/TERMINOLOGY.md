# Terminology

## Entity of Interest

The real object, service, system, study, product or other entity whose outcome matters.

## Domain Life-Cycle Profile

A domain-tailored view of an Entity of Interest across its relevant life cycle. Internally this may still be called an `E2E`, but `E2E` does not imply a standalone software subsystem.

## E2E

Internal shorthand only: the complete end-to-end life-cycle view of a defined Entity of Interest.

## Cross-Cutting Discipline

A discipline such as security, quality, safety or accessibility whose concerns apply across multiple life-cycle stages and domains.

## Enabling Capability

A capability used by one or more domain life cycles but not itself the final entity of interest in that context. Examples may include network access, compute, artifact conversion, distribution and agent execution.

## Knowledge Source

An authoritative or useful source such as a standard, scientific body of knowledge, method description, algorithm reference, tool documentation, benchmark or validated prior result.

## Capability

A machine- or human-realizable ability with declared inputs, outputs, conditions, side effects and validation options. A Tool is one provider of a capability; the capability is not identical to a specific tool.

## Composition

A reusable arrangement of methods, capabilities, workflows and validators used to solve a problem class under explicit applicability conditions.

## Validator

A domain-appropriate mechanism that produces evidence relevant to an acceptance criterion. A validator may be automated, formal, empirical or human where unavoidable.

## Evidence

An observation or artifact supporting or refuting a claim about execution, conformance or real-world outcome. Evidence is not equivalent to semantic success unless an applicable acceptance rule says so.

## Common Core

The minimal, capability-neutral semantics and rules required to discover, contextualize, compose, invoke and verify mature external capabilities. The current catalog of disciplines and tools is not itself the Core.

## Active Working Set

The bounded set of knowledge, methods, standards, algorithms, tools, agents and execution services activated for the current problem. It is analogous to working memory: contextual, replaceable and releasable. It carries no architectural permanence.
