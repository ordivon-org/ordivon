# Commercial Strategy Debt Externalization R1

Date: 2026-09-19  
Role: migration/currentness record, not an Ordivon strategy methodology.

## Debt retired

The following conflations are explicitly retired:

- enterprise operating model == company strategy;
- Business Model Canvas == strategy;
- capability breadth == market position;
- proprietary customer data == automatic moat;
- one successful automation == product candidate;
- provider/CRM records == demand;
- architecture completeness == company readiness.

## Adopted external owners

| Need | External/native owner | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| top-level strategic choice structure | Roger Martin Strategy Choice Cascade | structures choices; does not prove a winning position |
| customer/value/business-model hypotheses | Strategyzer VPC/BMC | hypotheses until buyer evidence |
| startup learning | Lean Startup validated learning / Build-Measure-Learn | experiments do not become evidence until real observations exist |
| early B2B selling | YC founder-led sales guidance | real conversations, quotations and paid work are the evidence |
| operating execution/governance | existing Enterprise Operating Model + natural provider/domain owners | how to operate, not where/how to compete |

## Forward rule

A new Ordivon-native strategy framework is inadmissible unless repeated measured work demonstrates a need not met by the mature external methods plus company-specific decisions/evidence.

The next useful evidence must come from market contact: candidate workflow comparison, customer discovery, a bounded first offer, founder-led sales, paid delivery, acceptance/outcome evidence and repeated economics.
