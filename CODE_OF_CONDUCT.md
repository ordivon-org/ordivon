# Contributor Conduct

Collaborate professionally and respect contributors with different
experience and backgrounds. Critique code, contracts, evidence and
ideas instead of individuals. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
publishing private information or retaliation are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate or restrict participation to protect a
productive technical environment. Raise technical disagreements in
Issues or PRs, and report sensitive conduct concerns to maintainers
through a private communication channel. For vulnerabilities, use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public discussion.

This conduct policy does not change ownership, licensing or product
authorization.
