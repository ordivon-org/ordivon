# Commercial Practice Externalization R1

Date: 2026-09-19
Role: migration record only.

## Replacements

| Previous/local abstraction pressure | Adopted external/native owner |
| --- | --- |
| customer-discovery Lens | Y Combinator early-stage guidance plus direct customer evidence |
| startup-learning lifecycle | Lean Startup Build-Measure-Learn and validated learning |
| customer/value/business-model Lens | Strategyzer Value Proposition Canvas and Business Model Canvas |
| outsourcing Lens | ISO 37500:2014 when outsourcing governance is material |
| company filing checklist as authority | current SAMR registration document/material specifications |
| ISO 9001:2015 quality baseline | ISO 9001:2026 for new current QMS work |
| custom business-record truth | ERPNext/Frappe for the business facts it naturally owns |

## Boundary

YC, Lean Startup and Strategyzer are advisory practices. They structure hypotheses and learning; they do not prove demand.

ISO 37500 is outsourcing guidance. The actual supplier arrangement still needs a concrete scope, responsibilities, evidence and commercial terms.

SAMR/current PRC rules and competent systems own PRC registration facts.

ISO 9001:2026 is the current QMS requirements edition; referencing it alone does not establish conformity or certification.

Customer acceptance, payment, retention and repeat business remain external evidence.

## Retention rule

Keep the founder or accountable decision owner exposed to early buyer conversations, first sales and pricing objections, problem selection, acceptance interpretation and product trade-offs.

Use Agents for bounded research, preparation, execution and analysis. Use external specialists for work where established professional capability is more appropriate than internal reinvention.

A new Ordivon commercial framework or Lens should be added only after a repeated, measured gap remains after using the appropriate external/native method.
