# Commercial Marketing Externalization R1

Date: 2026-09-19
Role: migration/currentness record, not an Ordivon marketing methodology.

## Decision

Do not create an `Ordivon Marketing Lens`, `Channel Score`, `SEO Score`, `Attribution Engine` or `Brand Gate`.

Bind each marketing/distribution responsibility to the mature external owner that actually governs it.

## External/native ownership

| Need | External/native owner | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| market/customer research | ISO 20252:2026 when research-service rigor is useful | research only; ISO explicitly excludes direct marketing |
| research ethics/professional conduct | ICC/ESOMAR Code 2025 | self-regulatory code; applicable law still controls |
| marketing communications | ICC Advertising and Marketing Communications Code 2024 | global self-regulation; jurisdiction/platform law/rules still control |
| organic Google search | Google Search Essentials | Google provider guidance, not a universal ranking standard |
| acquisition/source taxonomy | provider-native analytics such as GA4 source/medium/campaign | provider measurement, not causal proof |
| attribution reporting | provider-native attribution plus CRM/self-reported source | attribution model != experimentally identified causal effect |
| brand evaluation | ISO 20671-1:2021 | activate only for a real brand-evaluation decision |
| ad-tech privacy interoperability | IAB Tech Lab privacy standards | dormant until ad-tech integration; privacy law remains authoritative |
| paid-media/outcome measurement assurance | MRC standards | dormant until material paid-media measurement exists |
| channel selection | bounded market experiment | customer behavior decides; no standard can rank channels for Ordivon |

## Currentness observations

- ISO lists ISO 20252:2026 Edition 4 as published in September 2026.
- ICC identifies the 2025 ICC/ESOMAR revision as the fifth edition/current global research-code benchmark.
- ICC's current Advertising and Marketing Communications Code is the 2024 11th edition and explicitly covers AI/algorithms, digital/social marketing and related communication forms.
- Google Search Essentials remains the provider-native Google Search eligibility/best-practice source.
- GA4 currently exposes source, medium, campaign, campaign ID, source platform and channel groupings, with attribution behavior depending on scope/model.
- IAB/MRC are registered but dormant at the current pre-paid-media stage.

## Evidence boundary

```text
market-research report        != customer demand
impressions / followers       != qualified pipeline
search ranking                != revenue
GA attribution credit         != causal identification
CRM source field              != ground truth without reconciliation
marketing-code compliance     != effective marketing
content published             != distribution success
lead                          != qualified buyer
proposal                      != revenue
invoice issued                != payment
```

The commercial evidence chain remains:

```text
qualified conversation
  -> proposal/quotation
  -> paid delivery
  -> acceptance
  -> repeat / referral / expansion
```

## Current activation posture

Active now:

- founder-led outbound experiments;
- evidence-driven technical content;
- bounded technical-community distribution;
- self-reported/CRM channel provenance;
- source/medium/campaign tagging only where a web analytics provider is actually deployed.

Dormant now:

- paid ads;
- IAB ad-tech stack;
- MRC-level measurement assurance;
- brand-evaluation programme;
- influencer/sponsorship machinery;
- automated high-volume personalised outreach.

A dormant capability is not a missing architecture component.
