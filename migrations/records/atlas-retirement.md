# Atlas retirement

- Source: `/root/projects/ordivon-atlas`
- Final archive commit: `2692af95969d0e90894b6eecf188c4a7add85b27`
- Closed: 2026-09-14
- New-model disposition: **ARCHIVED HISTORICAL SYNTHESIS / NO CURRENT ATLAS OWNER**

## Decision

Atlas is no longer a current federated-currentness owner, research-start dependency, capability-map authority, or default prior-work retrieval path. Its source-fenced currentness/recovery experiments, manually curated synthesis, process lineage, acceptance evidence, and historical capability maps remain useful provenance and anti-rediscovery material.

Current cross-domain migration/classification belongs in Ordivon Next. Current facts come from concrete owner-native repositories/systems. Scientific literature and prior-art work belongs in `ordivon-research-v2` with mature scholarly sources/tools. Local historical recovery can use generic repository/file/library search over the preserved corpus.

## Why the current owner no longer justifies itself

The final Atlas configuration still described many owners that had intentionally become archived historical corpora, including Interlocus, Host v1, World, Normative, Computational Possibility, SCD, and Human. Preserving that registry as a live institutional/currentness surface would recreate stale architecture. `config/sources.json` is therefore retained only as the final pre-archive experiment configuration.

Atlas `first-look` itself was lexical substring/path matching over generated JSON projections and curated Markdown synthesis. Its own contract explicitly performed no semantic similarity, translation, query expansion, semantic-equivalence judgment, novelty judgment, or research admission. That residual does not warrant a permanent bespoke retrieval owner.

## Final consumer closure

Harness had already retired the packaged Atlas first-look Runtime bridge on 2026-09-09 (`19694d32e5d5effb8f3d2adfd00fda72265c2a4a`). A fresh census found only standalone, uninstalled Atlas research-start/dogfood scripts outside Harness package API/CLI/current docs. Those final surfaces were removed in Harness commit `72d47c5ba7264b0896c94392aa6254fd947903a6`; the Harness owner suite passed 417 tests after deletion.

No systemd unit, installed Atlas executable, or other current service dependency was found. Media's Atlas references are revision-bound book/source-map provenance and remain historical evidence.

## Verification

Atlas's default offline owner suite passed after archival: 108 tests executed with 0 failures and 45 explicitly opt-in live/remote tests skipped. The skipped suites remain available for historical reproduction; they are not current owner obligations.

## Future rule

Do not refresh Atlas as a prerequisite for ordinary research or project decisions. Reintroduce a specialized indexed projection only if measured retrieval/currentness failures persist after generic search, owner-native sources, Ordivon Next mappings, and mature Research tooling have been tried. Any admitted replacement should be scoped to the concrete residual rather than reviving Atlas as a broad institutional owner.
