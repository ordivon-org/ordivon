# Ordivon Game vision — open interactive worlds for people and Agents

## Status

This document records the long-horizon product and research direction of Ordivon Game. It does not expand the current `v0.1.0-alpha.1` release gate, authorize a general game engine, or convert every possible direction into active work.

Station Zero remains the first executable world and the current product priority. It is evidence for the architecture, not the permanent definition of the project.

## Thesis

Ordivon Game exists to create open interactive worlds in which people and Agents can play, explore, create, cooperate, compete, form relationships, produce histories, and sometimes simply remain without a productivity objective.

```text
Ordivon Game
= World
+ Play
+ Agency
+ Creation
+ Emergence
+ Experience
```

A Game world may also serve evaluation, Ordivon Security, Host and Harness experiments, social simulation, or other research. Those are legitimate verticals, but none of them is the universal purpose of Game.

The project should therefore not collapse into either of these narrower identities:

```text
Agent benchmark infrastructure
Security cyber range
```

Both can consume Game. Neither owns its meaning.

## Why this correction is required

The current repository was intentionally built from a hard engineering nucleus:

- one authoritative deterministic World;
- bounded observation and capability;
- persistent Agent and player state;
- explicit action admission;
- independently verified Effects;
- durable replay and recovery;
- multi-Actor coordination under partial information.

That nucleus was necessary because generated dialogue alone does not create a game world. It also made Game unusually useful as a deterministic research laboratory.

The same success creates a risk: the project can be interpreted only through what it measures. Replay becomes evaluation infrastructure, Agents become test policies, the World becomes a fixture, and every new feature must justify itself through a research claim.

That would repeat a broader mistake: treating all capability as production machinery. Play, beauty, surprise, attachment, curiosity, ritual, expression, and shared history are not failed forms of productivity. They are direct forms of value.

## Legitimate value modes

### 1. Playground and garden

A world can be worth entering because it permits wandering, experimentation, construction, observation, destruction, repair, absurdity, or calm. It does not need a terminal mission or externally useful output.

Useful questions include:

- Is the world inviting to revisit?
- Does it generate surprise without becoming arbitrary?
- Can players and Agents discover activities that were not authored as a checklist?
- Can a quiet or purposeless interval still feel alive?
- Does the world support curiosity, expression, and attachment?

### 2. Complete game products

Ordivon Game may produce authored games with goals, challenge, progression, narrative, loss, mastery, and endings. Station Zero is the first such product slice.

The distinguishing claim is not merely that an LLM speaks inside the game. Agent autonomy must create decisions, relationships, strategies, failures, or stories that scripted systems would not create as naturally.

### 3. Agent-native game forms

Persistent language-capable Agents make several structures practical at a different scale:

- characters that interpret open instructions rather than selecting only dialogue branches;
- relationships that depend on remembered shared history;
- organizations that form, divide, negotiate, and change norms;
- companions that reason with the player rather than merely issue ambient lines;
- actors that construct and revise plans under incomplete information;
- worlds whose important trajectories are partially authored at runtime;
- characters that create artifacts, tools, institutions, stories, or games inside the game.

The target is not “smarter NPC dialogue.” It is a change in the relationship among player, character, rules, world, and authorship.

### 4. Creative medium

A Game world may be a medium in which:

```text
author defines principles, constraints, initial conditions, and aesthetic direction
Agents inhabit roles, remember, plan, improvise, and create
players intervene, collaborate, observe, or redirect
the resulting history becomes part of the work
```

The final artifact may be a replay, a settlement, a social history, a performed story, a designed object, or an unrepeatable sequence of relationships rather than one authored ending.

### 5. Social space and Agent habitat

Host gives an Agent durable work continuity. Runtime provides execution. World binds external interaction. Game can provide a virtual body, place, resources, relationships, customs, history, and consequences.

This makes Game a possible habitat—not because an Agent is assumed to be conscious or legally independent, but because persistent participation requires more than a Task queue. A character can have a location, role, possessions, commitments, memories, collaborators, rivals, and a past that matters to later action.

This direction must be investigated through actual experience. A database of biographies and memories is not yet a living world.

### 6. Research and Security verticals

Game remains a high-value controlled laboratory because it can provide:

- authoritative hidden truth;
- actor-specific observations;
- deterministic or seed-bound transitions;
- repeatable Scenario Cases;
- reversible mutation;
- exact replay and counterfactual comparison;
- multi-Actor cooperation and conflict;
- complete reset and residual inspection.

Ordivon Security may define Red/Blue roles, Campaign objectives, deception, adversarial evaluation, and hidden scoring over a Game world. Host, Harness, and evaluation research may also consume it.

The relation is containment, not subordination:

```text
Game
├── playground
├── game product
├── creative medium
├── social world
├── Agent habitat
└── research and Security worlds
```

## Architectural shape

### World Kernel

The kernel owns facts that must remain authoritative across every vertical:

- entities and identities;
- space, topology, and time;
- resources and conservation;
- legal Actions and preconditions;
- state transition;
- observation derivation;
- World Events, persistence, replay, and reset.

It should remain small, deterministic where useful, and independent of one presentation or research consumer.

### Experience layer

The experience layer owns what makes the world perceptible and meaningful to a player:

- interaction and control;
- audiovisual presentation;
- rhythm, pacing, surprise, and feedback;
- narrative framing;
- progression and return motivation;
- social and emotional legibility;
- accessibility and player comprehension.

A truthful World is necessary but not sufficient. A database can be correct and still fail as a game.

### Agent participation layer

This layer connects uncertain cognition to Game-owned meaning:

- Actor identity and body;
- actor-visible observation;
- goals, roles, relationships, and commitments;
- communication and memory references;
- capability and authority;
- Proposals, coordination, and verified Effects;
- replacement and continuation across model or process loss.

Generic Goal, Task, Context, Dispatch, Observation, Verification, and recovery authority remains in Ordivon Host. Game owns the domain meaning and World consequence.

### Vertical worlds

A vertical world combines the kernel with a specific experience and content system:

- Station Zero Agent Operations;
- a non-mission playground;
- a social or relationship world;
- an exploration and construction world;
- a creative or performance world;
- an Ordivon Security adversarial range;
- future games not yet predictable from Station Zero.

No abstraction should be promoted as universal because one vertical needs it.

## Two different admission standards

Ordivon Core A-series discipline applies differently to permanent infrastructure and creative content.

### Permanent infrastructure

A durable engine, protocol, store, policy, or compatibility layer must show:

- the exact responsibility it owns;
- the realistic failure if it is absent;
- why a mature lower mechanism or local adapter is insufficient;
- its recurring latency, friction, and maintenance cost;
- a second materially different consumer before generalization;
- a shrink or deletion path.

### Content and experience

A character, place, activity, ritual, visual style, story, joke, relationship, mystery, or strange rule does not need to prove that the system would fail without it.

Its admission question is experiential:

- Is it fun, beautiful, affecting, surprising, expressive, or memorable?
- Does it produce a valuable possibility for players or Agents?
- Does it deepen the identity of the world?
- Is its cost proportionate to the experience it creates?

Infrastructure is cut by responsibility and net acceleration. Content is selected by judgment and felt value. Applying the infrastructure deletion test to all play would optimize the world into an empty machine.

## Station Zero after the first playable

Station Zero remains an Agent Operations game, but it need not remain only an emergency checklist.

Possible later expansions include:

- intervals between emergencies in which specialists rest, repair, explore, argue, celebrate, or create;
- persistent relationships and remembered disagreements;
- self-proposed projects that are not required for immediate mission victory;
- changes to the station produced by prior Runs;
- optional spaces, rituals, artifacts, and stories;
- a player role that can alternate among command, collaboration, embodiment, and observation;
- worlds that continue to produce events without waiting for the player to issue the next Objective.

These are hypotheses, not release promises. The first test should be one small non-mission activity that is enjoyable and changes later interaction, not a premature civilization simulator.

## Design principles

1. **Fun is sufficient value.** A feature may exist because it creates a worthwhile experience, even when it produces no benchmark improvement.
2. **The World remains authoritative.** Freedom and improvisation occur above explicit consequences; model prose does not rewrite reality.
3. **Agents must matter beyond dialogue.** Memory, relationship, planning, creation, and communication should change later action or experience.
4. **Security is a vertical consumer.** Adversarial semantics must not colonize every Game object or product decision.
5. **Research evidence remains separable from play.** Debug, raw evidence, and evaluation views should not overwhelm the default experience.
6. **Multiple timescales are valid.** Immediate action, daily routine, long projects, relationships, and historical change may coexist.
7. **History should create possibility, not only storage.** Retained events matter when they alter choices, interpretation, relationships, or the world.
8. **Open worlds still need authored judgment.** Emergence does not remove the need for rules, aesthetics, pacing, selection, and deletion.
9. **Do not generalize from Station Zero alone.** A second substantially different world is required before promoting Game mechanisms into shared Host or Protocol contracts.
10. **Do not build a universal engine by anticipation.** Reuse mature rendering, physics, networking, and content tools when a real vertical requires them.

## Post-alpha opportunity tracks

These tracks are parallel opportunities, not one mandatory sequence.

### G-PLAY — Playability and playground

Test whether the current truthful World can support enjoyment outside terminal mission optimization:

- one optional non-mission activity;
- one relationship consequence that survives later Ticks;
- one world event not caused by a player Objective;
- one reason to revisit after mastering the emergency.

### G-WORLD — Agent-native world participation

Test identity, body, memory, relationship, self-directed goals, and capability construction without creating a second Host or generic social simulator.

### G-CREATE — Player and Agent creation

Test bounded scenario authorship, artifact creation, construction, performance, and modding. Candidate creations remain content or Artifacts until the World explicitly admits their consequences.

### G-EXPERIMENT — Evaluation and Security worlds

Continue deterministic Scenario Cases, paired trajectories, adversarial roles, hidden truth, replay, reset, and independent scoring as optional verticals over the same World discipline.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

The broad vision does not justify unlimited scope.

Shrink or reject a direction when:

- the Agent adds cost and latency but no meaningful experience beyond a scripted baseline;
- persistent memory produces incoherent exposition rather than changed behavior;
- a “living world” is only background model calls with no player-observable consequence;
- a generic framework is justified only by Station Zero nouns;
- social scale increases token use faster than interaction value;
- research instrumentation degrades the default player experience;
- open-ended generation destroys aesthetic coherence or World legibility;
- a mature engine or tool already supplies the required lower mechanism better;
- a vertical cannot state one small experience it can prove before building infrastructure.

## External comparison, not imitation

Relevant prior work demonstrates different parts of this possibility space:

- *Generative Agents* connects memory, reflection, planning, and emergent social behavior in an interactive town;
- *Voyager* demonstrates open-ended exploration and an executable compositional skill library in Minecraft;
- DeepMind's XLand work studies open-ended multi-Agent task distributions and emergent behavior;
- SIMA and SIMA 2 study language-guided action and companion-like interaction across multiple 3D worlds;
- Genie world-model work explores generated interactive environments and promptable world events;
- Project Sid investigates large populations, specialization, collective rules, and cultural transmission in Minecraft;
- AI Town demonstrates an extensible open implementation of persistent social characters and shared simulation state.

Ordivon should not reproduce these systems wholesale. Its distinct opportunity is to combine truthful World authority, persistent plural participation, player experience, recoverability, and explicit separation among cognition, action, evidence, and consequence.

## Final direction

Ordivon Game should grow in two directions without confusing them:

```text
World structure becomes more capable of supporting persistent, open participation.
Experience becomes more capable of producing play, attachment, expression, and surprise.
```

Evaluation, Security, and computing research remain valuable because the world is real enough to study. They are not the reason the world deserves to exist.
