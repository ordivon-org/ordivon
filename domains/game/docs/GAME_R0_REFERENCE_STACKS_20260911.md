# R0 Representative Reference Stacks — 2026-09-11

## 0. Why stacks exist
The complete/bounded ranking census answers **what is successful on each source surface**. It does not answer what to copy or what caused success.

For every materially important archetype, R0-C separates:

```text
Flagship reference
  high-expression / high-scale evidence of what the form can become

Canonical mature reference
  stable interaction grammar worth understanding

Cheap causal baseline
  smallest lawful original-expression reproduction capable of testing one claim
```

Only the last layer is cost-filtered.

```text
ReferenceImportance != ReproductionCost
LargeProduct != LargeExperiment
TeardownAdmission != CloneImplementationAdmission
```

Exact machine-readable stacks are retained in `evidence/acceptance/game-r0-reference-stacks-20260911.json`.

## 1. First R1 teardown wave
The first wave is selected for **cross-form information coverage**, not because these are cheap games:

1. Counter-Strike 2 — competitive spatial mastery, lethal commitment, information and round structure.
2. Dota 2 — team strategy, role interdependence, economy/power curves and contested objectives.
3. Fortnite — battle-royale survival plus live/social/UGC product layering.
4. Minecraft — sandbox transformation, self-authored goals and survival/creation overlap.
5. Grand Theft Auto V — open-world systemic freedom, vehicle/action affordance and escalating world response.
6. ELDEN RING — telegraph/commitment/mastery, failure/retry and authored exploration.
7. Baldur's Gate 3 — reactive role-playing, multiple valid approaches and acknowledged consequence.
8. Factorio — automation, bottleneck diagnosis, expansion and self-produced complexity.
9. Mario Kart 8 Deluxe — racing feel, legibility, social competition and catch-up/chaos management.
10. Animal Crossing: New Horizons — low-pressure routine, self-expression, collection and return-time promise.
11. Candy Crush Saga — compact mobile puzzle sessions, near-miss/retry, progression and live product longevity.
12. Beat Saber — embodied timing, rhythm, spatial readability and rapid mastery feedback.

These twelve are **references to teardown**, not candidate products. They deliberately include products whose full production scope is far beyond a tiny team.

## 2. Access boundary
R1 can begin with public product observation, manuals, developer material, postmortems and recorded play, but experience/feel claims require appropriate direct Human play evidence.

```text
No local copy / no console / no VR today
!= reference deleted

No direct play evidence
= experiential standing remains pending
```

For example, Beat Saber remains an important VR/rhythm reference even if the current workstation cannot execute its intended embodied condition. Its desk teardown can proceed; claims about embodied feel cannot.

## 3. Huge-game micro-baselines
Examples of the corrected approach:

- Counter-Strike 2 → one graybox objective round; study position, information, lethal commitment and reset before reproducing an economy.
- ELDEN RING → one graybox duel; study telegraph → commitment → evade → punish → death/retry.
- Minecraft → break → obtain → place → transform; test whether a player forms a self-authored spatial intention.
- GTA V → one city block with vehicle possession and escalating law response; study systemic freedom without building a city.
- Baldur's Gate 3 → one authored situation with three mechanically distinct approaches and later acknowledgement.
- Mario Kart → one short track; separate basic racing feel from item/catch-up dynamics.
- Candy Crush → ten compact boards; separate core move/goal/retry grammar from monetization/live-ops layers.

The reproduction target is always the **causal claim**, never the commercial product's content volume.

## 4. Coverage still open
