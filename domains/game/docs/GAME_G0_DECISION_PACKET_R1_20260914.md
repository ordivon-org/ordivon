# G0 Decision Packet R1

## Final decision

```text
PRODUCT_SELECTED = false
G0_ENTERED       = false
DISPOSITION      = NO_COMMIT_CURRENT_PORTFOLIO
NEXT_STAGE       = PRE_G0_PRODUCT_THESIS_COMPOSITION_R2

```

The correct decision is **not** to force-rank a weak portfolio. The R1 thesis wave produced decision-relevant negative and partial evidence across all five alternatives. Entering G0 now would convert sunk cost, historical familiarity or one favorable proxy into product authority.

## Decision objectives

The decision preserves separate objectives rather than collapsing them into one score:

1. repeated decision value;
2. causal legibility;
3. robustness across bounded player/evaluator models;
4. production tractability;
5. mature-reference inheritance with explicit deliberate differences;
6. evidence efficiency / Value of Information;
7. anti-path-dependence.

## What the wave taught us

### T03 historical decontamination succeeded

The highest initial VoI experiment did exactly what it was supposed to do. A clean 6×6 simultaneous-commitment carrier removed delegation, Runtime Agents and dashboards. The resulting universal plan indicates that prior Station Zero attraction cannot be safely attributed to sealed tactical commitment alone.

### T02 remains the strongest partial survivor

T02 still has strong evidence that goals and perturbations move allocations and bottlenecks. However, the R1 product promise specifically foregrounded intentional topology redesign. With all baseline objectives sharing one topology, this version does not earn G0. This evidence should inform a future thesis, not trigger post-hoc tuning of the same one.

### T01 exposes a robustness boundary

The scarce-evidence mechanism is genuine: optimal and low-noise policies strongly outperform random evidence choice, while a generous information budget collapses the distinction. But the current product thesis becomes much less reliable as clue-selection rationality degrades. That is decision-relevant model sensitivity, not a reason to pretend the bounded player population is irrelevant.

### T04 exposes a real objective tradeoff

Responsive audiences produce strong adaptation and high creator diversity. The failure is subtler: at balanced creator/audience weighting the synthetic creators retain only about `67%` of their own-intent optimum, below the predeclared `75%` boundary. The current thesis has not shown authorship and adaptation coexisting strongly enough.

### T05 falsifies the old apparatus as the product carrier

PGP-A is useful apparatus and remains mechanically reusable, but the exact current traversal does not establish the T05 thesis under the declared synthetic motor-noise learner. Do not rebuild it merely to obtain a preferred answer.

## Value of Information after the wave

The major R1 uncertainties are no longer cheaply resolved by **more measurement of the same carriers**. The remaining uncertainty is increasingly about the product hypotheses themselves:

```text
more samples of T03 universal-plan carrier      -> low VoI
more tuning of T02 topology carrier             -> invalid post-hoc rescue
more T05 attempts on same geometry              -> low VoI
more T01/T04 simulation under one chosen model  -> model-relative refinement, not enough to justify G0
```

Therefore the value of **commit now** is lower than the value of returning to product-thesis composition, but the value of continuing the same R1 experiment wave is also low.

## Next-stage constraint

R2 must **not expand or modify the mechanism library** merely because all R1 product theses failed to earn commitment.

R2 may only:

- recombine already registered mechanisms;
- change product-level framing, player/context, cadence or mechanism coupling selection using existing vocabulary;
- inherit mature external patterns directly where appropriate;
- use the R1 negative regions as constraints;
- construct the smallest falsifier for genuinely new product-level hypotheses.

R2 must not:

- retune a failed R1 carrier until it passes;
- add complexity to rescue T02/T03/T05;
- loosen predeclared thresholds after seeing results;
- promote historical Station Zero, Casefile, PGP-A or D14 machinery into a product by familiarity;
- reopen Foundations/mechanism expansion without the existing explicit reopen condition.

## Preserved evidence

The underlying mechanism evidence remains reusable:

```text
D03 information scarcity creates evidence-selection value
D05 goals/perturbations create bottleneck/allocation change
D14 responsive deterministic audiences create differentiated consequences
CS2-03 information timing has player-model-dependent causal effects
PGP-A remains a mechanically valid movement apparatus
Station Zero remains historical/reference evidence
```

None of these facts selects a product by itself.

## G0 boundary

G0 remains unopened. A future thesis earns G0 only when decision analysis supports intentional commitment over both:

1. its live alternatives; and
2. the best remaining information-gathering action.

The R1 portfolio does not meet that condition.
