# Live Test-Account Admission — 2026-09-14

A user-designated live account is not automatically admitted as a test account. The admission evaluator consumes only fresh normalized authoritative reality plus permission/health evidence.

Admission requires:

- verified read-only reality;
- current clock gate pass;
- healthy reconciliation read path;
- trade permission present;
- withdrawal absent and transfer absent where exposed;
- no nonzero position;
- no open order;
- no non-quote nonzero asset balance;
- total quote balance at or below 1 quote unit.

An admitted account remains non-production. `productionTradingAuthorized`, withdrawals and transfers remain false.
