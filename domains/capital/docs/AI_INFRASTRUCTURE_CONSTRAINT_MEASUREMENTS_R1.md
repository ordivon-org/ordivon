# AI Infrastructure Constraint Measurements R1

Standing: **RESEARCH-ONLY measurement binding; not provider truth, not an admitted headroom model, not investment authority.**

## Why this layer exists

Recursive LEGO R2.1 says to revisit the minimal broken frontier rather than add reasoning depth everywhere. After the R1 graph was built, the broken frontier moved from representation to **measurement bridge quality**: most nodes had useful observations, but `capacity / requirement - 1` was not yet legal because units, scopes, time bases or denominator independence did not line up.

The measurement layer therefore asks before every ratio:

1. Are capacity and requirement the same physical/economic quantity?
2. Are they in the same unit?
3. Do they cover the same geography/product/configuration?
4. Do they cover the same delivery period?
5. Was the requirement measured independently, or was it itself generated by the supply-constrained model?

Only when all five pass may the graph promote a quantitative headroom ratio.

## What the method changed immediately

### China HBM

A naive calculation could divide Epoch's ~240k domestic-HBM-supported 950DT units by its ~320k total modeled HBM-equipped 950DT units and call the result `-25% headroom`.

R1 rejects that ratio. Both numbers are outputs of the same supply-constrained production model: the ~320k total is approximately 240k domestic-HBM units plus 80k units supported by modeled smuggled HBM. That denominator is not independent demand.

The useful measurements are retained instead:

- Huawei primary documentation: 950DT memory variants are 144GB/96GB at 4TB/s;
- Epoch median: domestic CXMT+XMC HBM supports ~240k 950DT 96GB units in 2026;
- model-implied allocated memory volume: 240k × 96GB = 23.04PB decimal;
- total modeled HBM-equipped 950DT slice: 320k × 96GB = 30.72PB decimal;
- Reuters: HBM scarcity coincided with 950DT indicated-price increases of roughly 20-50% over two months;
- Huawei chairman: AI-computing equipment supply is insufficient for China demand.

These jointly support `BINDING`/scarcity direction, but **not a defensible numerical China HBM headroom ratio**.

The next legal denominator is independently observed contracted/order demand by product and memory configuration, or a China-wide BOM-weighted HBM-GB requirement derived from independent accelerator orders.

### China accelerator logic

Huawei's statement establishes the sign `demand > supply`, while Epoch supplies a modeled 2026 production/output quantity. Without an independent same-unit demand number, numerical headroom remains `BLOCKED_REQUIREMENT_UNKNOWN`.

### U.S. transformer/switchgear

A reported high-voltage transformer lead time up to 160 weeks is a strong schedule-pressure measurement. It is not annual MVA supply or requirement. The next bridge needs matched voltage/specification-class production/import MVA, order backlog and delivery dates.

### U.S. grid/interconnection

Berkeley Lab's >2,060GW figure is a **generation+storage** interconnection queue. It cannot be divided into data-center load demand. ERCOT's June snapshot of >438GW tracked large-load requests (nearly 89% data centers) is load-side, and its March report also exposes a maturity funnel, but both remain regional and request/status data—not national deliverable headroom. R1 therefore requires a regional funnel:

`requested MW → studied/approved MW → contracted/energized MW → observed peak load`.
