## Pilot claim graph

```text
Reality
  ↓ accessed through
Representation / interface / measurement / query
  ↓ discriminating pressure produces
Evidence
  ↓ evaluated under source + scope + currentness
Standing
  ↓ does not itself authorize
Decision / commitment
  ↓ realized through an owner-appropriate action path
Effect / state transition
  ↓ observed through a claim-relative evaluator
Consequence / pressure
  ↘ updates standing/representation
   ↘ may also change future interfaces, affordances, authority, and problem space
```

This is a Book integration projection. It is not asserted as a universal closed-form epistemology.

## What is deliberately absent

- no universal scalar for confidence, value, maturity, learning, or Reality coupling;
- no global `ProblemState` or `ProblemGraph`;
- no automatic Book claim promotion from citations;
- no assumption that Human response is the terminal evaluator;
- no assumption that feedback implies causal gain;
- no assumption that the current representation spans all reachable discriminating pressure.

## Revalidation rule

Before a consequential Book revision, revalidate the exact upstream owner/source fences that carry the changed claim. Do not rebuild the whole research corpus merely because one chapter changes.
