# Ordivon Book Constitution v0

Status: `PILOT / NON-AUTHORITATIVE / SOURCE-FENCED`

This constitution governs the first Ordivon Book pilot. It is deliberately small. It does not create a new semantic owner, repository, database, schema, service, daemon, or MCP surface.

## 1. Purpose

The Book exists to answer a specific finite-intelligence problem:

> Can a Human or Agent recover Ordivon's current integrated model well enough to reason and act without replaying the research chronology that produced it?

The Book is therefore a **current explanatory projection**, not an archive and not a source of truth.

A successful Book reduces reconstruction cost while preserving the distinctions that change correct judgment, action, currentness, or recovery, and makes recoverable the bounded capability geometry those distinctions actually support.

## 2. Authority split

```text
Owner-native sources
    own domain facts, standing, authority, currentness

Atlas
    projects map, provenance, genealogy, history, recovery, currentness health
    and hosts explicitly non-authoritative cross-owner synthesis

Book
    selects, integrates, orders, explains, and exposes unresolved seams

Studio Writing
    owns the writing-medium craft used to realize the Book

Publisher (if any)
    owns publication identity, rendered reading surface, and distribution state
```

If Book prose conflicts with a current owner, the owner wins and the Book is stale or wrong.

`BookProjection != OwnerTruth`.

## 3. What the Book is not

The Book is not:

- a second Atlas;
- a replacement for owner-native current standing;
- a dump of Tasks or Results;
- an encyclopedia that attempts exhaustive coverage;
- a tutorial, command reference, API manual, or operational runbook;
- a persuasive narrative allowed to erase uncertainty or historical disagreement;
- a universal world model whose prose automatically governs action.

Tutorial/how-to/reference material stays with the systems that own those tasks. The Book concentrates on explanation and integrated current understanding.

## 4. Source and claim contract

Every load-bearing Book claim must have a recoverable path to one of:

1. owner-native current authority or evidence;
2. a source-fenced Atlas projection;
3. an explicitly non-authoritative synthesis whose scope ceiling is preserved;
4. a clearly marked external theory/method used only as comparative pressure.

The Book may create a new **integration statement**, but it may not silently upgrade the epistemic class of its inputs.

The following distinctions must remain visible when load-bearing:

`Observation != Evidence != Standing != Decision != Effect != WorldTruth`

`HistoricalValidity != Currentness`

`Projection != Authority`

`Recovery != Re-admission`

## 5. Currentness and revision

Book v0 uses the smallest currentness mechanism that already exists:

- exact Git source bindings in `production.json`;
- Git history for the Book artifact itself;
- claim-level source paths in `claims.json`;
- a bounded current cognition record.

No Edition schema is admitted yet.

The Book is reviewed or revised when at least one trigger fires:

- an upstream owner advances in a way that can change a retained Book claim;
- new discriminating evidence changes standing or exposes a missing distinction;
- a Human/Agent consumer cannot recover an action-relevant model from the projection;
- a source/currentness fence becomes unknown or stale;
- an intervention creates a materially new problem/consequence regime that invalidates the current chapter geometry.

A review may legitimately conclude `NO_CHANGE`.

Stable immutable editions become justified only when a real consumer needs stable citation, historical comparison, or simultaneous recovery of multiple Book states. Until then, Git revisions are sufficient provenance.

## 6. Chapter contract

A mature chapter should make the following recoverable without turning them into mandatory schema fields:

1. **Phenomenon / Reality pressure** — what makes the question matter?
2. **Distinctions** — what convenient identities currently fail?
3. **Current standing** — what is accepted, scoped, rejected, historical, or unknown?
4. **Constructed capability / reachability** — preserving those distinctions, what can the declared consumer now reliably distinguish, form, recover, route, decide, build, or reach that was not stable before, and what remains only candidate or conditional?
5. **Derivation / historical repair** — which changes of representation matter to understanding the current model?
6. **Practice / consequence** — what decisions, actions, systems, or problem spaces change?
7. **Reopen conditions** — what evidence or consumer failure would force revision?
8. **Source map** — where can a skeptical reader recover the load-bearing inputs?

The prose may reorder these for comprehension. The epistemic boundaries may not be removed for elegance.

## 7. Textual style v0

This is an epistemic writing profile for the Book pilot, not a universal Ordivon brand voice.

Prefer:

- phenomenon or distinction before project-specific vocabulary;
- one dominant argumentative move per paragraph;
- concrete pressure before abstraction when it improves discrimination;
- evidence and scope limitation near the claim they constrain;
- explicit changes of level: Reality, representation, standing, action, consequence;
- current and historical tense that cannot be mistaken for one another;
- endings that change the reader's model or expose the next unresolved pressure;
- when a chapter has earned one, an explicit capability gestalt near the ending that states the new reliable possibility together with its scope, maturity, and non-claim boundary;
- enough compression to make the whole model consumable, but no more.

Reject:

- chronology written as causality;
- narrative adjacency written as evidence;
- confidence of voice used to hide weak standing;
- metaphor that imports unearned system properties;
- smooth prose that makes a speculative synthesis feel settled;
- repeated terminology that hardens a local projection into an ontology;
- a single linear story that erases viable branches or rejected alternatives.

## 8. Consumer contract

### Fresh Agent

A fresh Agent should be able to read the current chapter and answer, without first replaying dozens of Tasks:

- What is the current integrated model?
- Which distinctions are load-bearing?
- Which parts are owner truth versus synthesis?
- What does the model permit or forbid inferring about action?
- What can the system or consumer now reliably distinguish, form, recover, route, build, decide, or reach that was not stable before, and under what scope/maturity boundary?
- Where should deeper provenance be recovered?
- What kind of Reality pressure would justify revision?

### Human

A Human should be able to read continuously and obtain a coherent mental model without needing internal repository ceremony. Human comprehension, taste, trust, or motivation are empirical Human-specific claims and cannot be declared proven by Agent review alone.

## 9. Failure modes

The Book must be revised or narrowed when it causes any of the following:

- **authority laundering** — synthesis wording is mistaken for owner truth;
- **coherence collapse** — a continuous story erases alternatives, scope, or historical repair;
- **currentness blindness** — readable prose remains stale after an owner changes;
- **causal narration** — temporal sequence is mistaken for mechanism;
- **terminal-result collapse** — a Result is treated as sufficient for every future consumer;
- **feedback collapse** — any observed feedback is treated as learning, gain, or truth;
- **consumer collapse** — Human readership is treated as the universal terminal of every Ordivon claim;
- **structure inflation** — prose pressure is answered by inventing new schema/services instead of fixing the representation;
- **capability occlusion** — load-bearing distinctions survive, but the chapter leaves the capability/reachability they jointly support difficult for the declared consumer to recover;
- **archive substitution** — Book growth becomes accumulation rather than integration.

## 10. Promotion and exit

This pilot earns stronger machinery only through repeated consumer pressure.

Possible future promotions include:

- immutable named editions;
- machine-checkable chapter dependency/currentness edges;
- structured claim references;
- additional Human/Agent projections over a shared source.

None are admitted by symmetry.

If a plain Writing Production plus source fences remains sufficient, the correct outcome is to keep it plain.
