## LEARNING

1. The earlier narrow Chapter-5 selection was not simply “wrong”; it was historically valid evidence produced under a method surface that had lost previously learned external-solution-preservation guards after lineage/owner changes.
2. Mature prior art must subtract false novelty without deleting solved solution content or the larger residual problem.
3. The genuinely non-redundant Chapter-5 object is not `Environment matters`; Chapter 1 already owns that. It is environmentalization admission: when a correct standing earns default status for a concrete consumer and where its responsibility belongs.
4. `Correct != ShouldBeDefault` is as important as `Standing != Action`: stable Knowledge may legitimately remain Knowledge-only.
5. Responsibility-relative primacy is strongest when it decomposes historical role bundles without creating a new universal center. Finance is a current implementation witness, not proof of one global allocation law.
6. Harness provides the key negative controls: exact reusable Knowledge need not be automatically injected, and First Interface can recompile visible affordances without minting truth, priority or authority.
7. Challengeability is what prevents successful sedimentation from becoming shadow authority. Re-grounding/demotion can change current placement while preserving historical validity.
8. Environmentalization must itself face consequence pressure; environment change is not benefit, and NO_CHANGE/no-default can be the mature result.
9. Structured-consumer carrier failures must remain separate from semantic standing. The final accepted consumers succeeded only after carrier questions were made discriminating rather than prose-heavy.
10. The five-chapter Book now reaches a coherent seam: finite intelligence forms scoped standing, consumes it through representations, acts under authority, recovers current action relevance over time, and can selectively compile repeatedly useful standing into a challengeable future environment—without claiming a closed theory of intelligence or a new central owner.
