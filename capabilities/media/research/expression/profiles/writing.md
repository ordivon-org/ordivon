# Writing expression profile

## Responsibility

This profile translates the shared Art & Expression core into sustained written argument, explanation, report, essay and related long-form forms. It owns **writing craft priors**, not the factual authority behind a claim and not the browser layout that later presents the text.

Current real consumers include Studio's `productions/runtime-introduction/story.mdx` and Ordivon Web's MDX publication system. Web owns its publication metadata, temporal status, navigation and rendered reading surface; this profile owns the more general writing-medium reasoning that can also transfer to scripts, reports, presentations or another publisher.

## What the medium can manipulate

Writing can deliberately control:

- claim order and information release;
- sentence length, syntax, cadence and emphasis;
- paragraph rhythm and conceptual chunking;
- section hierarchy and argumentative sequence;
- example, analogy, metaphor and counterexample;
- quotation, citation and evidentiary placement;
- explicitness versus interpretive openness;
- narrative distance, focalization and voice;
- repetition, callback, contrast and transition;
- title, deck, lead and ending as entry/exit structures;
- figures, tables, code and callouts when the publication medium supports them.

## Hard constraints

The following are stronger than prose preference when they apply:

- a sentence may not upgrade hypothesis, target, history or local observation into current fact;
- quotations, measurements and source-bound claims retain their evidence boundary;
- uncertainty and missing knowledge remain distinguishable from negative evidence;
- article/publication temporal identity is owned by the publication system that distributes it;
- a later project state does not silently rewrite what a dated article originally established;
- citations and claim boundaries are not rhetorical decoration: removing them must not change the apparent evidentiary class of the argument;
- generated summaries, social copy or excerpts must not become a second authority over the complete argument.

## Durable craft priors

These are `medium_prior` defaults, not universal laws:

- **plain model before internal vocabulary** — give the reader the phenomenon or problem before requiring project-specific nouns;
- **one paragraph, one dominant move** — a paragraph can contain nuance without forcing the reader to reconstruct several unrelated argumentative jobs;
- **evidence near the claim it constrains** — long distance between assertion and limitation encourages over-reading;
- **structure is rhetoric** — heading order, paragraph order and omission can imply causality, importance and certainty just as individual sentences can;
- **specific before abstract when comprehension matters** — concrete failure, mechanism or example can establish a model before abstraction generalizes it;
- **variation needs continuity** — cadence, examples and section shape may vary while the reader's argumentative model remains recoverable;
- **difficulty should belong to the idea** — unnecessary interface-like jargon or sentence complexity should not consume the reader's effort budget unless difficulty itself serves the work;
- **an ending should change the reader's model** — summary, consequence, unresolved pressure or next action should follow from the argument rather than merely stop the text.

## Common semantic failure modes

Audit the written artifact for at least these classes when relevant:

- chronology is written as causality;
- sequence or paragraph adjacency makes two facts appear evidentially dependent when they are not;
- confident voice hides weak or local evidence;
- metaphor imports properties the real system does not have;
- repeated wording makes one interpretation appear more strongly evidenced than alternatives;
- a title/deck overstates what the body later qualifies;
- current and historical tense blur product state;
- an omitted counterexample or limitation changes the apparent scope of a retained conclusion;
- compression removes the distinction between observation, interpretation, decision and world truth;
- fluent prose makes a speculative model feel settled merely because it reads smoothly.

## Render and inspection

Writing has at least two distinct artifacts:

```text
semantic text artifact
→ claims, argument, order, language, figures

rendered reading artifact
→ line length, typography, hierarchy, TOC, responsive behavior, fatigue, figures in context
```

The writing profile audits the first directly. A publisher such as Web must additionally audit the second through its own profile.

Useful inspection therefore includes:

- source/claim comparison;
- heading and argument-outline recovery from the finished text;
- search for status/epistemic drift;
- reading the complete article without author notes or rationale;
- rendered browser/page inspection when typography and figure placement materially affect meaning;
- targeted human/editor calibration only when the unresolved claim is actually about comprehension, voice, emotional effect or cultural interpretation.

Readability metrics may diagnose sentence or lexical patterns. They do not prove clarity, quality or artistic value.

## Protocol specialization

```text
FRAME
reader + intended model/experience + publication/encounter
    ↓
BIND
source claims + epistemic class + temporal state + focalization
    ↓
EXPRESS
argument + information order + voice + cadence + examples/figures
    ↓
RENDER
complete text, then real publication surface when applicable
    ↓
AUDIT
claim semantics + structural rhetoric + publisher-specific reading audit
    ↓
DECIDE
revise / no-op / promote
```

## Context signals

Current platform length preferences, fashionable tone, SEO conventions, social hooks, present audience vocabulary and genre trends are `context_signal`. They can materially affect distribution but should not silently become durable writing craft.

## Current local evidence

Ordivon Web's publication system already supplies useful bounded evidence:

- dated articles preserve their own argument while current Project/Question views can advance;
- `InBrief`, `PublicationFigure` and `ClaimBoundary` survived repeated flagship use, while unused proposed primitives were deleted;
- long-form rendering distinguishes desktop rail navigation from mobile TOC behavior;
- strong reports explicitly preserve “what the data does not establish,” demonstrating that limitation placement is part of the written argument rather than an appendix ritual.

These are production observations, not a universal house style.
