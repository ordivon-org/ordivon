# Expression profiles

Profiles translate the cross-medium creative core into faster-moving medium and encounter knowledge. They are intentionally downstream from [`../creative-system.md`](../creative-system.md), [`../protocol.md`](../protocol.md), and [`../knowledge-model.md`](../knowledge-model.md).

The registry is [`index.json`](./index.json).

## Ownership rule

A profile belongs where its irreversible medium facts and real production consumers live. Studio does **not** copy a profile merely to make the directory look complete.

Current C2 split:

- **Web** — owned by `/root/projects/ordivon-web/design/expression-profile.md`; Web binds the exact Studio Core it consumes. Studio records the ownership edge but does not mirror Web's browser/publication theory.
- **Motion / Video** — owned here in [`motion-video.md`](./motion-video.md) because Studio owns the current motion, video, NLE, media and QC production substrate.
- **Writing** — owned here in [`writing.md`](./writing.md) as cross-publication writing craft. Web remains authority for the browser publication surface and article temporal/publication state.
- **Still / Graphic, Audio / Music, Interactive** — remain in [`medium-baseline.json`](./medium-baseline.json) as provisional hypotheses until real production earns a stronger profile.
- **Spatial / 3D, Live / Realtime** — are candidate foundations in [`../media-topology.md`](../media-topology.md), not registry authorities yet. They must survive discriminating production and ablation before promotion.

The seven-foundation expansion program is tracked in [`../m7-foundational-media-program.md`](../m7-foundational-media-program.md). Its topology is deliberately falsifiable: a candidate that can be explained more compactly as a specialization, composition, encounter, distribution context, or tool adapter should not become a foundational profile.

## What a mature profile must answer

A useful profile stays compact and operational. It should make these things recoverable by an Agent:

1. **observer relation / irreducible affordance** — what materially different encounter or control relation justifies this profile;
2. **manipulable variables** — what the medium can actually change;
3. **hard constraints** — which medium or delivery facts are stronger than taste;
4. **craft priors** — durable professional defaults worth using or deliberately breaking;
5. **semantic failure modes** — how this medium can accidentally imply facts, causality, probability, state, location, agency or affect;
6. **render / inspect boundary** — what counts as the real artifact and how an Agent observes it;
7. **alternate-representation boundary** — what information/function is lost when the medium is unavailable and how accessibility/translation can preserve it;
8. **composition boundary** — what belongs to another foundation, specialization, encounter, distribution surface or tool adapter;
9. **falsifier and evidence state** — what would narrow, merge, demote or reject the profile and what real production evidence currently supports it.

A profile may also identify volatile `context_signal` inputs such as platform conventions or genre fashion. Those should be retrieved near a production rather than promoted into durable craft.

Profiles do not own source-domain truth, do not redefine the shared six-stage protocol, and do not become universal creative-control planes.
