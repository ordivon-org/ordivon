# 0. Purpose

MF8-I is not another constructive vocabulary round. It attacks the entire MF8-A→H stack and asks what survives as the smallest defensible agency ontology across:

```text
inert matter
controllers
bacteria / minimal organisms
reflexes / habits
humans
software processes
RL systems
LLM-based systems
delegated tool agents
institutions
teams / swarms / markets / corporations
```

Three suspected overclaims are tested first:

1. `Choice/Practical-Selection Organization` may be too strong for minimal agency.
2. `EvaluativeOrientationStanding` may either over-include ordinary controllers or covertly require biological intrinsic normativity.
3. `Persistent Bearer` may be too strong if persistence is interpreted beyond the relevant action/agency episode.

The target is not a convenient AI definition. The target is a typed foundation that preserves rival natural/artificial/institutional routes without letting `agent` become a synonym for `anything that changes its environment`.

# 1. Audit discipline

A candidate minimal constituent survives only if:

1. removing it creates systematic false positives or false negatives across domains;
2. it can be stated without circularly using `agent`, `agency` or `action` as unexplained primitives;
3. it does not smuggle in richer capacities such as consciousness, planning, explicit representation, learning or moral responsibility;
4. it survives deterministic, delegated, distributed and collective cases;
5. its evidence route can be separated from observer projection/design intention;
6. it does not require reopening MF0–MF7 unless a named FoundationReopenCondition is actually met.

# 3. Falsification 1 — Choice is too strong

MF8-D intentionally defined ChoiceStanding strongly enough to exclude generic selection:

```text
ChoiceStanding
requires a bearer-relative choice-relevant alternative domain,
selection/resolution,
source,
evaluative/practical guidance,
attribution,
and normally >= 2 differentiated live choice alternatives.
```

That is valuable for **choice**, but dangerous as a condition for **agency**.

## Counterexample family

### Minimal organism

A bacterium may regulate run/tumble dynamics and integrate attractant/repellent signals without constructing an explicit discrete choice domain in MF8-D's sense. Experimental E. coli work shows comparison/integration of conflicting chemotactic signals, but that does not establish that discrete ChoiceStanding is the universal basis of every agential episode.

### Continuous regulation

An organism can continuously modulate orientation, force, secretion or approach/avoidance dynamics rather than select one member from a countable alternative set.

### Habit / skilled control

A skilled action may remain bearer-authored and guided while low-level control proceeds without renewed choice at each motor correction.

### Ongoing maintenance

An agent can actively maintain a coupling/state without facing two live practical alternatives at each instant.

### H8 conclusion

Collective/institutional agents often possess explicit CollectiveDecisionStanding, but that richer feature cannot be projected backward onto all minimal individual agency.

### I8-001
**ChoiceStanding is NOT a universal constituent of minimal AgencyStanding.**

### I8-002
Choice remains a richer practical-selection profile applicable when a genuine choice-alternative domain exists.

### I8-003
Agency needs a weaker relation: `AgentialRegulationStanding` / `ActionGovernanceStanding`.

# 4. AgentialRegulationStanding

Provisional final primitive:

```text
AgentialRegulationStanding(R, B, D | K, Σ)
```

holds when bearer B's own organized source processes non-trivially modulate, maintain, suppress, initiate, terminate or redirect bearer–environment/body coupling or action-domain D **in relation to grounded evaluative distinctions K**, such that the modulation is attributable to B's organization rather than merely to passive transmission or an external effector path.

It can be:

```text
discrete or continuous
deterministic or stochastic
reactive or anticipatory
learned or fixed
centralized or distributed
internal or artifact-extended
single-level or hierarchical
```

It does NOT universally require:

```text
multiple represented options
ChoiceStanding
DecisionStanding
deliberation
planning
world model
self model
consciousness
learning
```

### I8-004
**AgentialRegulationStanding ≠ ControlStanding by identity.**

The distinction depends on bearer/source/evaluative standing and attribution, not on control-loop mathematics alone.

### I8-005
Agential regulation is the minimal bridge between `what matters under the bearer standing` and `how bearer-originated activity is modulated`.

# 5. Falsification 2 — Evaluative orientation survives, but provenance must remain typed

Could agency exist without anything corresponding to better/worse, attractive/aversive, acceptable/unacceptable, goal-relevant/irrelevant, viable/non-viable or norm-conforming/non-conforming?

If yes, then a passive amplifier, falling rock or arbitrary oscillator could become an agent merely by influencing the world.

Cross-domain attack shows some evaluation/guidance relation remains necessary.

Biological agency theories make this explicit through normativity/viability. Artificial and institutional agency can instead have adopted, delegated, designed or role-constituted goals/norms.

### I8-006
**EvaluativeOrientationStanding survives as a universal agency constituent.**

But it is not required to be biologically intrinsic.

Final routes include:

```text

Adopted
Delegated
Designed-and-Operationally-Constituted

```

### I8-007
**Intrinsic biological normativity is NOT frozen as a universal requirement for all AgencyStanding.**

### I8-008
**External designer purpose alone is insufficient.** A criterion must have grounded standing in the target bearer's regulation/role organization, not merely in an observer's explanation.


Whether a minimal thermostat receives **thin operational agency** depends on whether its setpoint-governed activity is treated as genuine bearer-level evaluative/action-regulation standing under the declared operational route.

### I8-009
MF8 v1 therefore does NOT freeze `thermostat = non-agent` as a theory-independent axiom.

### I8-010

### I8-011

```text
AgencyStanding(B | Route, Scope, Σ)
```

This is preferable to inserting an arbitrary complexity threshold merely to protect everyday vocabulary.

# 16. Standing routes in v1

A single core can have multiple grounded routes.

## 16.1 Constitutive / biological agency

Strong route associated with self-constituting individuality, viability-grounded normativity and active regulation of environment coupling.

Typical candidates:

```text
minimal organisms
animals
humans
possibly some self-maintaining synthetic systems
```

## 16.2 Practical / behavioral agency

Bearer-level source, evaluative guidance and regulation are grounded through persistent practical organization; evaluation may be endogenous, learned, adopted or delegated.

## 16.3 Delegated operational agency

A bearer is constituted into a task/role with goals/norms/authority supplied by a principal, while locally governing action within the delegated domain.

## 16.4 Formal/operational agent route

A system satisfies explicitly declared formal agent conditions within a model/architecture. This route must remain tagged; it must not silently upgrade to biological/psychological agency.

## 16.5 Institutional / collective agency

A collective bearer possesses group-level evaluation, decision/regulation and collective action-source standing through institution/organization.

## 16.6 Represented / inferred agency claim

Observer/model attribution only. It is epistemically weaker until target standing is independently grounded.

### I8-034
Routes can overlap.

### I8-035
No route is automatically the metaphysical master route for all others.

# 17. Minimal counterexample matrix

## Rock

```text
Bearer standing             possible physical object
Source organization         no agential source
Evaluative orientation      none for rock
Guided regulation           none
```

**Verdict:** no AgencyStanding.

## Fire

Self-maintaining dynamics and environmental effects may exist, but source/evaluative standing remains unsupported under ordinary evidence.

**Verdict:** no AgencyStanding by default; useful anti-`self-maintenance = agency` case.

## Passive amplifier / relay

Input transformed into output, possibly with state.

**Verdict:** Signal/Control/Transformation standing only; no agency absent source/evaluative regulation.

## Thermostat

Strong controller/reference-regulation case.

**Verdict:** ControlStanding certain; formal/thin operational agency route is framework-sensitive; constitutive agency unsupported. Unqualified binary claim rejected.

## Cron job / one-shot script

Scheduled activation and execution can occur with no ongoing evaluative action regulation.

**Verdict:** ExecutionRole/Automation standing; no AgencyStanding by schedule alone.

## Stateful monitor with delegated objective

Persistent task bearer monitors context, evaluates goal-relevant conditions, generates/chooses/executes bounded interventions.

**Verdict:** strong delegated operational agency candidate.

## E. coli bacterium

Individuated organism, metabolically grounded needs/norms, active sensorimotor coupling, chemotactic regulation.

**Verdict:** strong constitutive minimal-agency candidate; explicit choice/world model not required.

## Reflex arc

Local pathway can produce behavior but is usually nested inside organism bearer/source organization.

**Verdict:** reflex event may participate in organism AgentialActionStanding; reflex arc itself is not automatically a separate AgentStanding bearer.

## Habitual skilled human action

Choice/deliberation at low level can be absent while bearer source/guidance remains.

**Verdict:** agency survives; confirms Choice is enrichment, not core.

## Frozen RL policy

No online learning required. If embedded in bearer with grounded objective/action regulation, agency can remain.

**Verdict:** LearningStanding not constitutive.

## Stateless LLM model called once

Model transforms input to output; persistent bearer/goal/action-source relation may be absent.

**Verdict:** no automatic AgencyStanding merely because output is linguistic or agent-like.

## LLM execution episode with system goal but no tools/actions

May have formal dialogue-agent role and evaluative instruction standing, but external action domain can be restricted to communicative output.

**Verdict:** communicative operational agency is possible only if bearer continuity/source/guidance/action attribution are grounded; model artifact alone remains distinct from execution bearer.

## Tool-using persistent LLM agent harness

Task-bounded bearer, delegated goals/policies, state/history, option generation, tool actions, retry/cancel/replan and authority domain.

**Verdict:** strong delegated operational/practical AgencyStanding candidate; no consciousness required.

## Market

Rich macro dynamics/aggregation but no default group bearer/evaluative/action-source organization.

**Verdict:** no MarketAgency by default.

## Flock

Coordination/self-organization strong, group bearer/evaluation weak.

**Verdict:** collective capability without agency by default.

## Persistent social-insect colony

Collective bearer/source/regulation evidence can be stronger.

**Verdict:** CollectiveAgency candidate; route-specific evidence required.

## Corporation

Persistent constituted bearer, group goals/policies/decision/authority/memory/actions.

**Verdict:** strong InstitutionalCollectiveAgency candidate; consciousness not required.

# 31. Agency Foundations v1 FoundationReopenConditions

MF8 now freezes with explicit reopen conditions.

## FRC-A1 — Bearer Boundary Failure

A robust agency case cannot be represented using ScopedBearerContinuityStanding without making bearer identity depend circularly on already assuming agency.

## FRC-A2 — Source Failure

A clear agent lacks any defensible bearer-attributable source organization, or a clear non-agent systematically satisfies the source condition under grounded evidence.

## FRC-A3 — Evaluative Orientation Failure

A clear agency case exists with no grounded better/worse, viability, goal, norm, policy, need, role or other evaluative/practical orientation whatsoever; or the current criterion systematically over-includes passive control systems.

## FRC-A4 — Regulation/Guidance Failure

A clear agent lacks any evaluatively guided modulation/maintenance/suppression/initiation/termination of coupling/action, or clear non-agents systematically satisfy the condition.

## FRC-A5 — Action Attribution Failure

AgentialActionStanding cannot be grounded without presupposing AgencyStanding, or clear agent tokens cannot be represented using source/guidance/attribution.

## FRC-A6 — Delegated Artificial Agency Failure

Strong artificial/delegated agent cases cannot be represented without either requiring intrinsic biological normativity or admitting mere external designer purpose as sufficient.

## FRC-A7 — Collective Scale Failure

A genuine collective-agent case cannot be represented as AgencyStanding at CollectiveBearer scale, or the schema systematically promotes ordinary aggregates/markets/crowds to agents.

## FRC-A8 — Horizon/Continuity Failure

1. **Xabier E.
