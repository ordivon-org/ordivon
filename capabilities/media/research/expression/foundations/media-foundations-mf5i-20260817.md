# 0. Purpose

MF5-I is not another subtype round. It attacks the entire provisional Space model produced by MF5-A→H.

The two primary failure modes are:

```text
Over-inclusion:
nonspatial sets / metrics / graphs / timelines / embeddings / state systems

Under-inclusion:
projective, topological, region-based, graph, virtual, configuration,
perceptual, fuzzy or nonmetric spatial domains are excluded by an
```

The decisive question is:

> **What is the smallest non-circular invariant that distinguishes spatial standing from merely having mathematical structure?**

MF5-I finds that the original MF5-A candidate was close but insufficiently protected against structural over-inclusion. `Topology`, `metric`, `adjacency`, `betweenness`, `coordinates` and `reachability` are all too structurally generic: the same formal structures can realize temporal, causal, semantic, social, computational or spatial roles.

The required reconstruction is **Positional Standing**.

Spatial, temporal, semantic, causal and other domains can share abstract mathematics without sharing ontological role.
