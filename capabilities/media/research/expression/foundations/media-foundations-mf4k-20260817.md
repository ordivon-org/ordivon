# 0. Purpose of MF4-K

MF4-K is not another positive-domain expansion.

It attempts to destroy the strongest MF4-J candidate:

```text
Composition
  ≈ plurality
  + grounded typed organization
  + nontrivial joint structure
  + counterfactual whole relevance
  + scope
```

The attack set includes:

1. analyst-defined arbitrary sets/relations;
2. weak physical aggregates;
3. continuous fields without object atoms;
4. transient perceptual/dynamic groups;
5. broken/degraded compositions;
6. contradictory/frustrated systems;
7. overlapping/polyhierarchical wholes;
8. recursive/self-relational structures;
9. environmentally open systems;
10. never-instantiated specifications;
11. spontaneous active coalitions without standing design;
12. random graphs/global statistics;
13. correlated common-cause variables;
14. multimodal co-presence without binding;
15. event lists without narrative organization;
16. pseudo-interactive broadcasts/generative streams;
17. arbitrary coarse-grainings that predict in-sample;
18. equally valid incompatible decompositions;
19. relation perturbations with collateral effects;
20. predictive global descriptors without causal standing;
21. robust systems whose semantic identity changes;
22. fragile but genuine compositions;
23. parts whose identity depends on the whole;
24. formal compositions whose relations are never physically enacted;
25. active organizations with no prior type/specification;
26. borderline cases where composition seems graded.

The target is not to preserve MF4-J. The target is to freeze only what survives.

# 1. Falsifier A — arbitrary analyst-defined sets

Take unrelated entities `a,b,c` and define:

```text
S = {a,b,c}
R = "all selected by analyst at 17:00"
```

`R` is real as a proposition about the analyst's selection act.

But the target entities do not thereby acquire a new physical, functional, semantic, perceptual or operational whole organization beyond being objects of that external selection.

### Failure found in MF4-J language

Calling this relation merely `grounded` is ambiguous: it is grounded in an analyst event, yet that does not ground composition *of the selected target entities under the asserted target scope*.

### Reconstruction K-01

**Composition needs organization standing in the target system/practice/history/specification under the declared scope, not merely truth of an externally imposed predicate.**

This is stronger than `relation exists` and more precise than generic `grounding`.

---

# 2. Grounding was mixing ontology and epistemology

MF4-J used `grounding` for both:

- why a relation has standing at all;
- how an analyst knows that it does.

These are distinct.

### Reconstruction K-02

Introduce:

```text
OrganizationStanding(O | Σ)
```

for the **ontic/constitutive establishment route** of `O` under scope.

And separately:

```text
EvidenceForStanding(Ev -> O | Σ)
```

for epistemic support.

### K-03

Possible standing routes include:

- physical/dynamical coupling;
- construction/assembly history;
- design/specification;
- convention/institutional rule;
- perceptual organization;
- representational/semantic grounding;
- functional recruitment;
- causal/developmental history;
- active interaction-generated organization.

No route is universally necessary.

### K-04

**Evidence route ≠ standing route.**

A perturbation experiment may be evidence for a dynamical standing relation; a specification file may be evidence for a designed standing relation.

---

# 3. Falsifier B — weak physical aggregates

Consider:

- grains in a pile;
- books in a box;
- nearby stars selected by coordinate window;
- droplets temporarily adjacent.

If composition required only physical proximity/contact, nearly every aggregate would count in the same way.

But rejecting all aggregates is also wrong: a pile has whole-level geometry/stability/load relations; a stack can have order/contact support relations; a box can impose containment and packing constraints.

### Reconstruction K-05

**Aggregate ≠ composition universally, but aggregation can instantiate composition when relations among members nontrivially organize joint state/shape/support/order/function under scope.**

### K-06

A weak aggregate may have a weak/limited composition profile rather than no composition whatsoever.

### K-07

`Composition` is deliberately broader than `designed system`, `organism`, `artifact`, `representation` or `integrated agent`.

---

# 4. Falsifier C — continuous fields with no pre-given object atoms

Turing's reaction–diffusion morphogenesis model begins with continuously distributed chemical concentrations and shows that interactions/diffusion can destabilize a homogeneous condition and produce patterned spatial organization.

The ontology cannot require pre-existing discrete object parts.

### Failure found

`Plurality of individuated units` is too object-like if read discretely.

### Reconstruction K-08

Replace discrete plurality with:

> **differentiated multiplicity**

which may consist of:

- discrete parts;
- regions;
- degrees of freedom;
- modes;
- states/events across time;
- roles;
- field distinctions that become relationally individuated.

### K-09

**Composition does not require object atoms.**

### K-10

A completely undifferentiated scalar considered at one point is not yet a composition under the same scope; composition begins when relationally relevant distinctions/multiplicity are available.

---

# 5. Falsifier D — one physical object with internal/self-relations

A recursive process, folded polymer, feedback controller or self-referential symbolic object may be `one object` at one granularity while containing internally differentiated states/roles/regions at another.

### Reconstruction K-11

**Physical object count is not the plurality criterion. Internally differentiated multiplicity is sufficient.**

### K-12

Self-relations are admissible only when argument roles/states/times are genuinely differentiated; trivial `x relates to x because x=x` does not establish composition.

---

# 6. Falsifier E — transient groups

A chord lasts milliseconds; a perceptual grouping may appear briefly; two moving bodies can transiently enter a coordinated relation.

Persistence cannot be constitutive.

### K-13

**Composition existence does not require persistence.**

### K-14

Persistent identity claims require additional history/equivalence criteria; transient compositions only require organization standing during their lifetime.

---

# 7. Transient composition vs accidental coincidence

Two unrelated flashes can happen simultaneously.

A chord's tones are temporally/harmonically organized; a coincidence may have no organization-sensitive consequence beyond analyst selection.

### Reconstruction K-15

**Duration does not distinguish composition from coincidence; organization standing and organization-sensitive joint profile do.**

---

# 8. Falsifier F — broken/degraded compositions

A broken engine may lose a critical dependency. A corrupted document may violate syntax. A failed protocol run may violate a transition rule.

If all composition-defining relations had to be currently satisfied, failure would destroy composition identity immediately.

That contradicts ordinary and technically useful token identity.

### Failure found

`Current nontrivial organization` alone is too strong for degraded tokens.

### Reconstruction K-16

Separate:

```text
CompositionStanding
CompositionRealization
CompositionIntegrity
CompositionActivation
```

### K-17

A token may remain historically/type-continuous with a composition while some expected relations are currently broken.

### K-18

**Degraded composition ≠ absence of composition.**

### K-19

Eventually enough identity-defining relation/history loss can make token identity fail; there is no universal damage threshold.

---

# 9. Falsifier G — frustrated/contradictory wholes

Spin-glass frustration, contradictory narratives, competing controllers and adversarial games show that whole organization does not require all local constraints/goals to be satisfiable simultaneously.

### K-20

**Joint organization ≠ global harmony.**

### K-21

Constraint conflict can itself have standing as a relation profile.

### K-22

Coherence remains evaluative/typed and is not constitutive of composition existence.

---

# 10. Falsifier H — overlapping/polyhierarchical wholes

One component may belong to:

- multiple software modules/views;
- multiple perceptual groups;
- multiple communities;
- several narrative/theme structures.

Palla et al.'s overlapping-community work is a canonical hard case against universal disjoint partition assumptions.

### K-23

**Composition does not require a unique partition.**

### K-24

Overlapping and cross-cutting compositions can be simultaneously real under different grounded relation profiles.

### K-25

Non-uniqueness ≠ arbitrariness.

---

# 11. Falsifier I — equally valid incompatible decompositions

Suppose decomposition `D1` is best for fault isolation while `D2` is best for causal prediction and `D3` for human interpretation.

MF4-J's alternative-decomposition comparison could be read as demanding one winner.

That is too strong.

### Reconstruction K-26

**Alternative-decomposition comparison is an epistemic discriminant, not a constitutive requirement for composition.**

### K-27

Several decompositions may each have objective standing relative to different relation/evaluation scopes.

### K-28

Claims must not silently generalize a scope-specific decomposition into a universal one.

---

# 12. Falsifier J — open systems

A conversation depends on language/community; software depends on OS/network; a living system exchanges energy/matter; a game session depends on services/users.

### K-29

**Environmental closure is not constitutive.**

### K-30

A composition boundary is a typed selective boundary/interface, not a metaphysical isolation wall.

### K-31

External dependencies belong in the composition profile rather than disqualifying the whole.

---

# 13. Falsifier K — standing design never instantiated

A score never performed, circuit never built, protocol never run or blueprint never constructed can still contain highly specific relations.

But saying the intended machine `exists as a physical composition` would be wrong.

### Reconstruction K-32

Distinguish:

```text
CompositionSpecification / CompositionType
CompositionToken / Realization
ActiveCompositionEpisode
```

### K-33

An uninstantiated specification can itself be a composed representational artifact and can specify a composition type, while no token realization of the specified system exists.

### K-34

**Standing specification ≠ realized token.**

---

# 14. Falsifier L — spontaneous active organization without prior specification

A transient coalition, spontaneous perceptual grouping, self-organized physical pattern or emergent coordination may have no prewritten type/design.

### K-35

**Pre-existing specification/type is not necessary for token composition.**

### K-36

Organization standing can arise endogenously from current dynamics/history.

---

# 15. Falsifier M — random graphs and impressive global statistics

Erdős–Rényi random graphs can display global properties such as connectedness and threshold behavior without those properties implying designed semantic/functional unity.

However, a formal random graph is itself unquestionably a mathematical composition: vertices and generated edges are part of its formal construction.

### Failure found

Saying `random graph is not a composition` would conflate two scopes:

1. the formal graph object;
2. an external physical/social interpretation inferred from graph statistics.

### Reconstruction K-37

**Formal relational construction can establish formal composition even when the relation assignment is random. Randomness ≠ absence of composition.**

### K-38

**A global graph statistic does not by itself establish that an external substrate forms a functional/semantic/causal whole of the same kind.**

### K-39

Representation/model composition and target-domain composition remain distinct under MF3.

---

# 16. Falsifier N — common-cause correlation

Two measurements can correlate because of a shared external source while having no direct functional/binding relation to one another.

### K-40

**Correlation/dependence alone is insufficient for the asserted world-level composition.**

### K-41

The variables may nevertheless be components of a larger causal/measurement composition that includes the common source and observation relations.

### K-42

Composition claims are relation- and scope-specific rather than absolute labels on entity sets.

---

# 17. Falsifier O — multimodal co-presence

Audio and video can share a file/time window while being unrelated.

MF4-G already established:

```text
Co-presence ≠ alignment ≠ correspondence ≠ common cause ≠ integration ≠ fusion
```

### K-43

**Common container/carrier is insufficient for cross-modal composition at the claimed semantic/source/integrative level.**

### K-44

The file itself can still be a container/artifact composition at another scope.

This is a crucial lesson: failing a stronger composition profile does not imply absence of all weaker composition.

---

# 18. Falsifier P — event list without narrative

An ordered list of events is an ordered composition as a list artifact.

It may nevertheless fail to be a narrative composition if causal/intentional/discourse/context relations are absent.

### K-45

**`Not narrative` ≠ `not composition`.**

### K-46

Composition is layered/profiled: the same token may instantiate ordered-list composition while failing narrative composition.

---

# 19. Falsifier Q — pseudo-interactive broadcast/generative stream

A prerecorded or unconditioned generative stream can be richly composed but noninteractive if participant state does not contingently alter future system transition/output.

### K-47

**`Not interactive` ≠ `not composed`.**

### K-48

Interaction is a specialized dynamic composition profile, not the minimal definition of composition.

---

# 20. Falsifier R — arbitrary coarse-graining that predicts in-sample

A high-dimensional system admits many partitions/projections. Some will fit historical data by chance.

MF4-J's `scope stability` could be read as constitutive, but a one-off artifact/transient composition need not generalize out of sample.

### Reconstruction K-49

**Transformation/generalization stability is evidence quality for claims of persistent/general structure, not a universal constitutive condition of composition.**

### K-50

Out-of-sample/interventional stability is especially important when using a learned/statistical decomposition to infer target-domain standing.

### K-51

A one-off composition can be real without statistical generalization if its relations are independently established by construction/design/history/current dynamics.

---

# 21. Falsifier S — counterfactual relevance and redundant relations

Suppose two redundant links independently preserve function. Removing either one changes nothing; removing both breaks the whole.

If MF4-J requires each composition-defining relation to be individually necessary, redundancy becomes impossible.

### Failure found

Per-edge necessity is too strong.

### Reconstruction K-52

**Counterfactual relevance applies to organization/substructure/profile collectively, not necessarily to each relation token individually.**

### K-53

A relation can be composition-relevant through:

- necessity;
- sufficiency contribution;
- redundancy;
- overdetermination;
- conditional relevance;
- enabling/blocking role;
- role assignment;
- error tolerance.

### K-54

No individual-edge ablation criterion is universally necessary.

---

# 22. Falsifier T — counterfactual tests with collateral effects

Deleting a module may remove data, power, timing and interface simultaneously.

### K-55

**Physical ablation is an evidence method, not a constitutive ontology condition.**

### K-56

Matched permutation/substitution can provide cleaner evidence but may still be impossible for unique historical artifacts.

### K-57

Structural/formal/normative counterfactuals are admissible evidence where physical intervention is unavailable.

---

# 23. Falsifier U — predictive global descriptor with no causal standing

A macro statistic can predict a system without being an intervention variable or component.

### K-58

**Causal manipulability is not necessary for a descriptor to be useful, but descriptor usefulness does not make the descriptor itself composition-defining organization.**

### K-59

Composition relations need not all be causal; designed, formal, semantic, conventional and perceptual standing remain admissible.

---

# 24. Falsifier V — robust system whose semantic identity changed

A translated/dubbed/transcoded artifact can preserve function while changing authorship/content/style; an error-correcting system can preserve output while hidden representation changes.

### K-60

**Robustness/invariance always names a preserved profile; it does not establish total identity.**

### K-61

Identity is typed by the declared profile and history/convention where relevant.

---

# 25. Falsifier W — fragile but genuine composition

A cryptographic packet, exact temporal pattern, one-shot arrangement or unstable physical structure can be destroyed by tiny perturbation.

### K-62

**Robustness is optional. Fragility does not falsify composition.**

---

# 26. Falsifier X — part identity depends on whole

A role such as `subject`, `bass note`, `server leader`, `figure`, `narrative protagonist` may only be individuated relative to larger organization.

If parts had to be fully individuated before composition, circularity appears.

### Reconstruction K-63

**Unit individuation and whole organization can be mutually constraining/recurrent.**

### K-64

The ontology does not require a strict bottom-up order:

```text
pre-existing parts -> relations -> whole
```

### K-65

A stable composition analysis may solve units/boundaries/relations jointly.

---

# 27. Falsifier Y — `whole-level profile` is potentially circular

MF4-J used `whole-level profile Φ` inside the definition of composition.

If `whole-level` simply means `belongs to a composition`, this is circular.

### Failure found

Terminology must be reconstructed.

### Reconstruction K-66

Replace `whole-level profile` in the constitutive criterion with:

> **organization-sensitive profile**

A profile is organization-sensitive when admissible contrasts in relation/order/binding/layout/constraint organization can change its identity, value, interpretation, capability, trajectory or evaluation while relevant constituent inventory/local properties are held fixed where meaningful.

### K-67

After composition standing is established, such a profile may legitimately be called a whole-level profile.

---

# 28. Falsifier Z — `nontrivial restriction` is too narrow

Some composition relations primarily:

- assign roles;
- create reference;
- establish ordering;
- permit transitions;
- add an interpretation;
- define evaluation;

rather than merely reduce the cardinality of physical state space.

### Reconstruction K-68

Replace `nontrivial restriction` with:

> **nontrivial joint organization**

meaning the relation structure makes distinctions among joint configurations, roles, transitions, interpretations or evaluations that are not exhausted by independent coexistence of the differentiated elements.

### K-69

Restriction/forbiddance is one subtype of joint organization, not the universal form.

---

# 29. Falsifier AA — scope stability was over-constitutive

A transient percept, one-off artwork or one historical event can be compositionally organized at one exact scope without surviving broad transformations.

### Reconstruction K-70

**Explicit scope is constitutive to the claim; stability across larger transformation classes is an evidence/identity/generalization property, not universal existence criterion.**

---

# 30. Falsifier AB — alternative-decomposition comparison was over-constitutive

A clear manufactured device can have obvious standing even if no alternative decomposition is tested.

### Reconstruction K-71

**Alternative-decomposition comparison is required when ambiguity/cherry-picking is a live epistemic risk, not for every composition existence claim.**

# 31. Does composition itself come in degrees?

Perceptual grouping strength, relation probability, activation, integrity and evidence confidence can all be graded.

But collapsing them into one `degree of composition` hides distinct questions.

### K-72

Do not freeze one universal scalar composition degree.

### K-73

Keep separate:

- standing confidence;
- relation strength;
- realization completeness;
- activation level;
- integrity/coherence;
- boundary certainty;
- membership probability;
- evidence quality.

### K-74

Borderline composition claims can remain uncertain/profile-dependent without a universal degree scale.

# 32. Final reconstruction of CRC

MF4-J CRC mixed constitutive criteria and evidence rules.

Final v1 separates them.

## Ontological core

### A. Differentiated multiplicity

There are at least two relationally distinguishable units/regions/states/events/roles/degrees of freedom under scope, not necessarily discrete physical objects.

### B. Organization standing

Some typed organization among those distinctions has standing in the target system/practice/history/specification/dynamics/perception rather than only in an analyst's arbitrary set predicate.

### C. Nontrivial joint organization

The organization establishes distinctions among joint configurations, roles, transitions, interpretations or evaluations beyond independent coexistence.

### D. Organization-sensitive relevance

At least one declared profile of the organized multiplicity depends on the organization under admissible contrasts. This is collective/structural relevance; no individual relation token must be uniquely necessary.

### E. Scope

All of the above are indexed to a declared granularity/context/consumer/evaluation scope.

# 33. Final Composition Foundations v1 definition — FROZEN

> **A composition is a scope-relative organized multiplicity—discrete or continuously differentiated—in which typed relations, constraints, bindings, orderings, mappings, interfaces or role structures have standing in the target system, practice, history, specification, dynamics or perception rather than only in an analyst's arbitrary grouping; this organization nontrivially structures joint configurations, roles, transitions, interpretations or evaluations, and at least one declared profile of the organized multiplicity is sensitive to admissible contrasts in that organization.**

This definition deliberately does **not** require:

- representation;
- semantics;
- conscious perception;
- agency;
- purpose;
- active causal integration;
- environmental closure;
- perfect coherence;
- constraint satisfaction;
- robustness;
- emergence;
- persistence;
- unique boundaries;
- unique decomposition;
- discrete object parts;
- centralized control;
- one global descriptor.

```text
CompositionStanding = <
```

# 36. Final realization schema

```text
CompositionRealization = <
  StandingRef? : optional composition type/specification,
```

# 37. Composition specification / token / active episode

```text
CompositionSpecification
 ≠ CompositionToken
 ≠ ActiveCompositionEpisode
 ≠ CompositionIntegrity
```

- Specification/type: defines roles/relations/constraints/possibilities.
- Token/realization: concrete instantiation/history-bound candidate whole.
- Active episode: relations/processes currently enacted/recruited.
- Integrity: degree/profile of current satisfaction relative to standing/type/identity criteria.

# 38. Final organization-standing routes OS1→OS8

## OS1 Physical/dynamical

Organization fixed by material interaction, topology, field coupling or dynamics.

## OS2 Construction/assembly

Production history establishes part/order/interface relations.

## OS3 Designed/specification

A score, protocol, program, blueprint or schema specifies relational standing.

## OS4 Conventional/institutional

Public practices/rules establish roles, ordering, reference or permissions.

## OS5 Perceptual

A perceptual system organizes evidence into a grouping/object/scene/event profile.

## OS6 Representational/semantic

MF3 grounding establishes standing-for/reference/content relations among composition roles.

## OS7 Functional/systemic recruitment

A system treats the organized multiplicity as one unit/process/resource for operation/control/evaluation.

## OS8 Endogenous active/history-generated

Current interaction/self-organization produces a relation structure without prior specification.

Hybrid standing is common.

# 39. Evidence rules are now separate from ontology

Potential evidence:

- physical/dynamical measurement;
- controlled intervention;
- relation permutation;
- matched substitution;
- component hold-fixed/context change;
- design/specification records;
- production provenance;
- conventional/public practice;
- perceptual grouping/behavior;
- operational addressability;
- interface/dependency traces;
- predictive/generalization tests;
- failure/recovery patterns;
- historical continuity;
- alternative decomposition comparison.

### CFV1-E1

No one evidence route is universally required.

### CFV1-E2

Evidence for organization standing must be matched to the claimed standing route/profile.

### CFV1-E3

Correlation, proximity, connectedness, shared labels, common containers and global statistics are weak/ambiguous evidence unless typed standing/relevance is independently established.

### CFV1-E4

Relation permutation/substitution is especially strong where constituent confounds can be held fixed.

### CFV1-E5

Physical intervention is not mandatory for historical/designed/formal/conventional compositions.

### CFV1-E6

Alternative-decomposition comparison is required when multiple plausible decompositions make arbitrariness a material risk.

### CFV1-E7

Evidence quality/confidence is not composition existence itself.

# 40. Final Composition Foundations v1 axioms CFV1-01→CFV1-64

**CFV1-01 Scope indexing** — Composition claims are relative to declared granularity/context/evaluation scope.

**CFV1-02 Differentiated multiplicity** — Composition requires internally distinguishable multiplicity, not necessarily discrete object count.

**CFV1-03 Non-objectual admissibility** — Regions, fields, states, events, modes and roles can compose.

**CFV1-04 Mutual individuation** — Unit/boundary and whole organization may constrain one another recurrently.

**CFV1-05 Typed organization** — Generic `related-to` edges are insufficient; relation/constraint/binding/order/mapping roles must be typed enough for the claim.

**CFV1-06 Organization standing** — Organization must have standing in the target system/practice/history/specification/dynamics/perception beyond analyst-only selection.

**CFV1-07 Standing ≠ evidence** — Constitutive establishment and epistemic evidence are distinct.

**CFV1-08 Hybrid standing** — Several standing routes may jointly establish one composition.

**CFV1-09 Nontrivial joint organization** — Organization distinguishes joint configurations/roles/transitions/interpretations/evaluations beyond independent coexistence.

**CFV1-10 Tautology rejection** — A relation true under every relevant configuration contributes no discriminative organization by itself.

**CFV1-11 Organization sensitivity** — At least one declared profile depends on organizational contrast.

**CFV1-12 Collective relevance** — Organization sensitivity applies to relation structures/substructures; every edge need not be individually necessary.

**CFV1-13 Redundancy admissibility** — Redundant/overdetermining relations can remain composition-relevant.

**CFV1-14 Negative constraints** — Prohibitions/separation/interface blocks can be composition-defining.

**CFV1-15 Standing/realization distinction** — Composition specification/type and token realization are distinct.

**CFV1-16 Active/standing distinction** — Active enactment is not required for standing composition.

**CFV1-17 Spontaneous composition** — Active composition need not have a prior design/specification.

**CFV1-18 Integrity distinction** — A token can remain a composition while some expected relations are broken/degraded.

**CFV1-19 Failure threshold typing** — Loss of identity under damage is profile/history dependent, not universally scalar.

**CFV1-20 Persistence optionality** — Transient compositions are admissible.

**CFV1-21 Identity typing** — Identity-across-change requires declared invariants/equivalence/history criteria.

**CFV1-22 Part turnover admissibility** — Same composition may survive constituent replacement under preserved profile.

**CFV1-23 Relation-change sensitivity** — Same constituent inventory can instantiate a different composition after binding/order/layout/dependency change.

**CFV1-24 Overlap admissibility** — Components may belong to several compositions simultaneously.

**CFV1-25 Polyhierarchy admissibility** — One unique tree/partition is not required.

**CFV1-26 Multiple valid decompositions** — Distinct grounded decompositions can coexist across scopes.

**CFV1-27 Non-uniqueness ≠ arbitrariness** — Plurality of valid analyses does not license unsupported partitions.

**CFV1-28 Environmental openness** — External exchange/dependency is compatible with composition.

**CFV1-29 Closure optionality** — Physical, perceptual, operational, causal and narrative closure are typed optional profiles.

**CFV1-30 Coherence optionality** — Composition existence does not require global/local coherence or consistency.

**CFV1-31 Conflict admissibility** — Frustrated, competitive, contradictory local relations can belong to one composition.

**CFV1-32 Constraint satisfaction optionality** — Not all intended/local constraints need be simultaneously satisfied.

**CFV1-33 Robustness optionality** — Fragile compositions are admissible.

**CFV1-34 Robustness ≠ identity** — Perturbation tolerance and identity are separate profiles.

**CFV1-35 Emergence optionality** — Whole-level novelty/capability is not constitutive.

**CFV1-36 Representation optionality** — A composition need not represent anything.

**CFV1-37 Semantics optionality** — Meaning/reference are not universal prerequisites.

**CFV1-38 Agency optionality** — Agents/controllers are not universally required.

**CFV1-39 Purpose optionality** — Shared function/goal is not universally required.

**CFV1-40 Causal integration optionality** — Standing designed/formal compositions can exist without active current causal coupling.

**CFV1-41 Common-cause insufficiency** — Shared causal ancestry alone does not establish the asserted composition.

**CFV1-42 Statistical dependence insufficiency** — Correlation alone does not establish compositional standing.

**CFV1-43 Proximity insufficiency** — Spatial/temporal proximity is a cue/profile, not sufficient standing.

**CFV1-44 Container insufficiency** — Shared file/page/box/channel does not establish stronger semantic/functional composition.

**CFV1-45 Connectedness insufficiency** — Graph/topological connectedness alone does not establish target-domain wholeness.

**CFV1-46 Global-statistic insufficiency** — Mean/entropy/order parameter/global descriptor does not by itself constitute the whole.

**CFV1-47 Descriptor ≠ cause** — Macro summary/intervention roles require separate evidence.

**CFV1-48 Randomness admissibility** — Randomly assigned formal relations can form a formal composition; randomness is not non-composition.

**CFV1-49 Formal/target distinction** — A composed model/graph does not imply the represented substrate has the same composition structure.

**CFV1-50 Layered composition** — One token can be a composition at a weaker layer while failing a stronger specialized profile (e.g. ordered list but not narrative; artifact but not interactive).

**CFV1-58 No universal composition scalar** — Evidence confidence, relation strength, activation, integrity and coherence must not be collapsed into one degree of composition.

**CFV1-59 Evidence plurality** — No single empirical/formal evidence method establishes all composition claims.

**CFV1-60 Counterfactual discipline** — Organization-sensitive claims should support admissible contrastive reasoning; physical ablation is only one possible evidence route.

**CFV1-62 Alternative-analysis discipline** — Plausible competing decompositions should be compared when analyst arbitrariness is a real risk.

**CFV1-63 Provenance discipline** — Observed, designed, inferred, conventional and generated organization claims must preserve their provenance/status.

# 43. Final failure taxonomy

## Arbitrary selection inflation

Analyst-selected set treated as target-system composition without target-scope standing.

## Relation-type collapse

Generic edge used where causal, functional, representational, temporal, spatial or normative role matters.

## Standing/evidence collapse

Evidence method mistaken for constitutive relation or analyst hypothesis treated as established organization.

## Independent-coexistence inflation

Members share location/container/time but no nontrivial joint organization at claimed level.

## Layer overclaim

A weaker composition profile is used to claim a stronger one: list→narrative, file→multimodal integration, output stream→interaction.

## Per-edge necessity fallacy

Redundant/overdetermined organization rejected because single-edge ablation has no effect.

## Ablation confound

Relation removal also changes constituents/resources so whole relevance is not isolated.

## Unique-decomposition fallacy

One valid analysis treated as the only real decomposition.

## Arbitrary-pluralism fallacy

Existence of several valid decompositions used to license unsupported ones.

## Closure inflation

Environmental sealedness/perceptual closure/narrative completion treated as universal wholeness.

## Coherence inflation

Quality/consistency/harmony treated as composition existence.

## Robustness inflation

Perturbation tolerance treated as identity/wholeness.

## Emergence inflation

Any macro effect treated as irreducible new ontology.

## Descriptor reification

Summary statistic/order parameter treated as component or cause without evidence.

## Formal-target conflation

Composition of graph/model/representation transferred to target world without grounding.

## Persistence inflation

Transient composition rejected for lacking longevity.

## Integrity/identity collapse

Broken token immediately declared nonexistent or historical identity retained despite loss of all relevant criteria.

## Object-atom assumption

Continuous/internal relational organization rejected for lacking discrete parts.

## Scope laundering

Composition status at one granularity/profile asserted universally.

## Provenance collapse

Designed/inferred/perceived/observed organization claims mixed without status distinction.

# 44. Final falsification protocol for future composition claims

For a new `X is a composition` claim:

1. **Name scope.** What granularity/context/profile is asserted?
2. **Name differentiated multiplicity.** What distinctions play units/regions/states/events/roles?
3. **Name typed organization.** Which bindings/orders/constraints/interfaces/etc. matter?
4. **Name standing route.** Why does that organization belong to the target system/practice/history rather than analyst selection?
5. **Name organization-sensitive profile.** What identity/capability/content/trajectory/interpretation/evaluation depends on the organization?
6. **Separate specification/token/activity/integrity.** Which existence claim is being made?
7. **Choose evidence route.** Perturbation, history, specification, convention, perception, operation, etc.
8. **Use relation-preserving controls when possible.** Hold constituents fixed; permute/substitute organization.
9. **Check alternative layers/decompositions.** Failing one specialized profile does not entail no composition; passing one weak profile does not prove stronger profiles.
10. **Preserve uncertainty/provenance.** Do not promote inferred standing into observed fact.

# 49. FoundationReopenConditions — MF4

Reopen Composition Foundations only if at least one concrete condition appears:

## FRC-C1 — zero-multiplicity counterexample

A compelling composition is found with no internally distinguishable parts/regions/states/events/roles/degrees of freedom under any relevant scope.

## FRC-C2 — analyst-only composition counterexample

A target-system composition claim is objectively warranted even though its organization has no standing beyond an analyst's arbitrary predicate/selection under the asserted scope.

## FRC-C3 — organization-insensitive whole counterexample

A composition is objectively identifiable although no admissible organizational contrast could affect any identity/capability/content/trajectory/interpretation/evaluation profile of the organized multiplicity.

## FRC-C4 — universal closure necessity

Cross-domain evidence shows one specific closure type is necessary for all genuine compositions.

## FRC-C5 — universal causal/informational integration necessity

A strong theorem/empirical result establishes active causal or information integration as necessary across formal, designed, perceptual, narrative and standing compositions.

## FRC-C6 — standing/realization collapse

A domain demonstrates that specification/type and realization/token cannot be coherently distinguished while retaining composition identity.

## FRC-C7 — redundancy failure

Collective organization sensitivity cannot handle robust redundant/overdetermined compositions without reintroducing per-edge necessity or arbitrariness.

## FRC-C8 — continuous-field failure

Generalized differentiated multiplicity still excludes a widely accepted continuous/process composition in a nonterminological way.

## FRC-C9 — arbitrary decomposition leakage

A family of analyst-created decompositions systematically satisfies CFV1 standing + organization-sensitivity criteria without any meaningful target-system/practice/history distinction.

## FRC-C10 — layer-collapse failure

Specialized profiles such as narrative/interaction cannot be treated as stricter compositions over a common minimal base without contradiction.

## FRC-C11 — evidence/ontology inseparability

A mature domain demonstrates that organization standing cannot be distinguished from evidence for standing without losing explanatory power.

## FRC-C12 — identity failure

Broken/partial/transforming compositions cannot be handled by standing/token/integrity/equivalence profiles across multiple domains.

Absent these or similarly specific falsifiers, do not reopen MF4 merely because a new composition example appears.
