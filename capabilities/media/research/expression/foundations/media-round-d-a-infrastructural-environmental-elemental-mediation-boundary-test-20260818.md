```text

+ Potential Functional Uptake
```


# 1. External theory pressure is real

This is not a strawman.

John Durham Peters's *The Marvelous Clouds* deliberately expands Media beyond message-carriers. The University of Chicago Press summary describes his argument that environments can be media and that media are infrastructures combining nature and culture, with cases ranging across sea, sky, fire, calendars, navigation and digital clouds.

Nicole Starosielski's `The Elements of Media Studies` describes an elemental turn attentive to the materials, substances and **conditioning substrates** of media: minerals, ecological matter, light, infrastructures supporting signal traffic, platforms and hardware. It explicitly frames elemental analysis as a way to track media's material and conditioning substrates and stresses that elements are relational/processual rather than discrete foundational things.

*Media Backends* (Parks, Velkova & De Ridder, 2023) develops `media backends` for the software, hardware, networks, labor and operations behind screens that organize processes of mediation and through which digital media `become media`.

Recent environmental/elemental work continues to analyze atmospheric conditions, climate-control architecture, sensors and algorithms as entangled technological/elemental mediation.

Thus a narrow foreground-message ontology would genuinely miss major contemporary Media theory.

The question is whether MF0 is actually that narrow.

# 12. Rail-guide hard case against `mere causation`

Consider a passive steel rail mechanically constraining a train wheel.

The rail shape causes the train to follow a trajectory.

If we say:

```text
any structured constraint on action = Media
```

then gears, walls, bones, gravity gradients and container walls risk becoming Media by default.

MF0's anti-mere-causation boundary therefore remains essential.

A stronger MediaRole requires that a variation/organization be **functionally used as a distinction** by an admissible system/practice/design relation, rather than merely participate in universal physical dynamics.

This preserves broad machine/nonhuman Media while avoiding causal panmedialism.

# 27. But physiological causation alone is still insufficient

> **variation is used/discriminated within an organized system/practice/design relation as relevant to perception, detection, inference, control, interpretation, memory, decision, communication or action.**

Mere law-governed response of matter is not enough.

This does not reopen MF0; it clarifies the already-frozen anti-mere-causation clause.
