# 0. Purpose

MF9-D showed that GNW, recurrent-processing, higher-order and predictive/inferential theories can be compared mainly along access, recurrence, higher-order and inference axes.

Integrated Information Theory (IIT) is structurally different. IIT does not merely propose that a functional architecture correlates with consciousness. IIT begins from claimed essential properties of phenomenology, translates them into physical postulates, and proposes an **explanatory identity** between an experience and the intrinsic cause–effect structure of a maximal substrate.

MF9-E therefore asks:

> **Can IIT's phenomenological axioms, physical postulates, intrinsic-causal formalism and exclusion rule be represented without collapsing them into Ordivon's lower-level Composition, Information, Causality, Unity or Bearer concepts—and what would actually falsify the resulting constitutive bridge?**

IIT 4.0 (Albantakis et al. 2023) preserves the overall program but substantially revises the formal realization.

