# 0. Purpose

MF9-K found that the Experience programme was missing an entire axis.

A→J mostly compared:

```text
which system / state / mechanism / architecture
supports or constitutes ExperienceStanding?
```

But major mind–matter ontologies ask a different question:

```text
what is the metaphysical status of experience,
what grounds what,
and what makes one phenomenal subject rather than many?
```

The two dimensions must not be collapsed.

MF9-L therefore constructs a **GroundingStack** orthogonal to MF9-D's mechanism-level `TheoryStack`.

Its goal is not to prove physicalism, dualism, Russellian monism, panpsychism, cosmopsychism or idealism.

The goal is narrower and foundation-first:

> **Identify the distinctions every theory-neutral Experience Foundation must preserve even when mind–matter grounding and subject composition remain unresolved.**
