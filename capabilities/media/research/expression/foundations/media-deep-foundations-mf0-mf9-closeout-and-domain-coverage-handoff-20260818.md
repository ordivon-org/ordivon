But **interaction as an encounter between multiple agents/users/media systems**, with turn-taking, mutual modelling, common ground, norm formation and interface conventions, has not had its own whole-domain audit.

## Embodiment

Touched through perception, body/peripersonal space, agency and experience.

But embodiment as a broad media/cultural/social/technological relation has not been independently audited.

## Collectivity

Touched through composition, macro dynamics, collective agency and candidate collective experience.

But group formation, social identity, publics, crowds, audiences, institutions and culture remain incompletely explored.

## Computation

Touched heavily as falsifier and realization regime in MF1/MF3/MF5/MF6/MF7/MF8/MF9.

But computation itself has **not** been reconstructed as a Media foundation referent. The historical label `MF11 Computation` is only an old roadmap hypothesis.

## Network / distribution

Touched through channels, topology, timing, distributed boundaries, RTP/media synchronization, collective dynamics and AI systems.

But Ordivon now also has an independent Network Deep Foundations project. Therefore future Media work must distinguish:

```text
Network-owned transport/topology/communication reality
```

from:

```text
Media-specific circulation/distribution/publication/availability/reach/encounter consequences
```

before claiming a Media foundation.
