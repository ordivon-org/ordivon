# 0. Purpose

MF9-D/E audited Global Workspace, Recurrent Processing, Higher-Order, Predictive/Inferential and IIT routes. MF9-G then showed that any universal theory must survive transfer beyond report-capable adult humans.

Several major consciousness traditions remain structurally homeless:

```text

Sensorimotor Contingency Theory (SMCT)
Broader enactive / radical-embodiment approaches
Affective / homeostatic / brainstem-centered routes
Strong and weak illusionist / functional-reconstruction routes
```

These cannot be compressed into D/E without losing their central claims.

MF9-H therefore asks:

> **What exact explanandum does each remaining route target; what does it claim is necessary or sufficient; which parts are functional architecture, constitutive bridge, biological realization, or ontological revision; and which cross-bearer hard cases would falsify it?**

### H9-003

# 3. AST core claim

Graziano & Webb's canonical formulation is roughly:

```text
selective attention
→ requires flexible control
→ control improves through an internal simplified model of attention
→ the model represents self + attention + attended object/state
→ the system attributes awareness to itself
```

The attention schema is compared to a body schema: simplified, useful and incomplete rather than a literal copy of mechanism.

### H9-004
AST's strongest functional claim is:

```text
AttentionSchema
→ better endogenous attention control
```

### H9-005
Its stronger consciousness claim is:

```text
Awareness
= information represented in / by the attention schema
```

These two claims must remain separate.

# 4. AST explains attention–awareness dissociation by model error

Because a schema can diverge from what it models:

```text
Attention without awareness
```

```text
Awareness-like representation without matching attention
```

become possible.

### H9-006
This is structurally valuable because MF2 already froze:

```text
Attention ≠ Awareness
```

AST offers one mechanism for why they correlate but dissociate.

### H9-007
AST therefore **consumes** MF2's distinction rather than reopening it.

# 5. AST 2016 behavioral evidence

Webb, Kean & Graziano (2016) used metacontrast masking to compare attention with versus without subjective awareness of a cue.

They found attention could still be drawn by an unseen cue, but in the absence of awareness:

```text
attention was less stable over time
inhibition of return was reduced
attention was more driven by luminance contrast
```

### H9-008
This supports:

```text
Awareness-associated processing
→ improves some dimensions of attention control
```

### H9-009
It does **not uniquely establish**:

```text
AttentionSchema
= ExperienceStanding
```

because other access, recurrent, working-memory or control mechanisms can potentially explain the same behavioral deficits.

# 6. TPJ evidence is theory-relevant but not theory-exclusive

Later AST work linked awareness-dependent control effects to right temporoparietal junction and related networks.

### H9-010
A region participating in awareness/control is not identical to an attention schema by anatomical fiat.

Need:

```text
representation content
causal role
specificity
competitive model comparison
```

not merely localization.

# 7. AST neural-network agent is a decisive layer-separation test

Wilterson & Graziano (2021) implemented a deep-Q-learning agent controlling a visuospatial attention spotlight.

With access to a descriptive attention schema:

```text
learning/control improved strongly
```

and disabling the schema after learning impaired performance.

### H9-011
This is direct proof-of-principle that a simplified model of attention can materially benefit an attention controller.

### H9-012
It is **not proof that the artificial agent had ExperienceStanding**.

The experiment itself therefore sharpens:

```text
FunctionalUtilityOfAttentionSchema
≠ PhenomenalSufficiencyOfAttentionSchema
```

# 11. AST versus illusionism

### H9-018
**AST ≠ strong illusionism.**

One can accept an attention-schema mechanism while remaining a phenomenal realist.

```text
SensorimotorContingencyStanding(SMC,B,Modality|ActionClass,SensoryTransformation,Σ)
```

### H9-025

# 31. Damasio: feelings as source/ground of subjectivity

Recent Damasio formulations propose continuous homeostatic feelings as the bridge that identifies mental contents as belonging to a particular organism.

A 2024 formulation describes three processes:

```text
1. continuous interoceptive feelings
2. continuous sensory images from organism-relative perspective
3. combination of feeling + perspective producing subjectivity
```

### H9-051
This is more specific than saying `body matters`.

It proposes:

```text
continuous homeostatic feeling
→ organism ownership / subjectivity grounding
```

### H9-052
The strong version therefore predicts that a system lacking the relevant homeostatic-feeling organization lacks ordinary subjective consciousness even if it performs high-level cognition.

# 32. Solms: elemental consciousness as affect

Solms' free-energy formulation identifies the elemental form of consciousness with **affect**, locating its core mechanism in extended homeostasis associated especially with upper brainstem systems.

In simplified form:

```text
unpredicted homeostatic deviation / uncertainty
→ affective feeling
→ valenced guidance of action
```

### H9-053
This makes a very strong necessity claim:

```text
Affect
is foundational to all consciousness.
```

### H9-054
Cognitive/perceptual consciousness becomes elaborated upon an affective conscious base rather than the other way around.

# 33. Merker: upper brainstem as consciousness integrator

Merker (2007) argues that upper brainstem systems integrate:

```text

action selection
motivation
```

into the serial coherent organization required for behavior, while cortex elaborates conscious contents.

### H9-055
Merker's thesis is not simply:

```text

```

It gives upper brainstem a more constitutive role in the conscious state.

### H9-056
The theory is supported by comparative, decortication and hydranencephaly observations, but those cases infer experience from behavior and surviving organization rather than directly demonstrating phenomenal sufficiency.

# 34. Brainstem lesions are powerful necessity evidence for state support

### H9-058


# 43. Illusionism is different: it attacks the explanandum

Strong illusionism is not merely another mechanism claiming:

```text
M causes experience
```

Frankish distinguishes:

```text
radical realism

strong illusionism
```

Strong illusionism proposes:

```text
real sensory / cognitive states
+
introspective self-representation
→ systematic representation of those states as phenomenally qualitative
```

while denying the represented classic phenomenal properties are actually instantiated.

### H9-074
Illusionism therefore operates partly at the **explanandum-selection layer** of MF9.

# 44. Strong versus weak illusionism

Introduce:

```text
PhenomenalPropertyRealismStanding(P|PropertyConcept,Σ)
QuasiPhenomenalPropertyStanding(Q,B|CausalRole,IntrospectiveEffect,Σ)
PhenomenalityRepresentationStanding(PR,B|TargetState,Content,Σ)
```

Frankish's distinction:

```text
Weak illusionism / weak realism:
  experiences really have qualitative properties,
  but introspection misrepresents them as ineffable/intrinsic/private/etc.

Strong illusionism:
  experiences do not instantiate phenomenal properties at all;
  introspection responds to merely quasi-phenomenal physical/functional properties.
```

### H9-075
**PhenomenalPropertyRealism ≠ belief that ordinary conscious states exist.**

Strong illusionists still accept real sensory/cognitive states and an `inner life` in a functional/introspective sense.

# 46. Illusion problem

Strong illusionism replaces:

```text
Hard Problem:
Why do physical processes generate phenomenal properties?
```

with:

```text
Illusion Problem:
Why do physical/introspective processes represent us as having phenomenal properties?
```

### H9-078
A successful illusionist theory must explain at least:

```text
phenomenal judgements
introspective certainty
modality-specific qualitative distinctions
apparent privacy/intrinsicality
richness/continuity intuitions
affective motivational force
why the representation is stable and adaptive
```

not merely assert `qualia are fake`.

```text
misrepresentation / disposition to phenomenal judgement
```
