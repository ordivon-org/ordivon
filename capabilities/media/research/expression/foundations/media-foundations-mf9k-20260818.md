# 5. Why panpsychism is not merely another constitutive mechanism

Canonical constitutive panpsychism proposes, in broad form:

```text
fundamental physical entities / fundamental reality
already instantiate phenomenal properties
or primitive forms of experience.
```

Human/animal macroexperience must then arise through some relation of:

```text
combination
constitution
grounding
fusion
subsumption
```

rather than experience emerging from wholly nonexperiential ingredients.

David Chalmers' panpsychism work explicitly distinguishes panpsychism, panprotopsychism and Russellian-monist alternatives, and identifies the combination problem as the central burden.

### K9-005

```text
When does nonexperience become experience?
```

into:

```text
How are fundamental experiential/phenomenal ingredients
related to the macroexperience of a subject?
```

That question was not answered in A→J.
