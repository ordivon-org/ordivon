## Truth-role firewall

Do not collapse these layers:

- **Foundation authority** — MF0–MF9.
- **Derived research** — typed profiles/relations that survived as real but nonprimitive.
- **Coverage history** — searches, destructive tests and closure evidence.
- **M7 production profiles** — historical/consumer knowledge packages.
- **Art & Expression** — Studio-owned adjacent research on aesthetics, narrative, rhetoric, style and creative validity.
- **Cross-owner truth** — owned by its source project; Media stores only bridge/navigation statements.
- **Engineering consumption** — future use of research, not research authority.

`Profile` is therefore not one global namespace: Round-B/D profiles, M7 production profiles and profile fields internal to Foundation documents have different truth roles.
