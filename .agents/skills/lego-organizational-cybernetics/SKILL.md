# LEGO Organizational Cybernetics Lens

Use this lens when many semi-autonomous units must act locally while remaining viable as a larger whole.

## Diagnostic coordinates

Use VSM functions as questions, not forced component names:

1. Operations — which autonomous units perform the primary work?
2. Coordination — what dampens oscillation/conflict among operations?
3. Control — what allocates shared resources and assures current internal performance?
4. Intelligence — what observes the external environment/future and proposes adaptation?
5. Policy/Identity — what preserves purpose, identity, and balance between present operations and future adaptation?

Also inspect recursion: each operational unit may itself contain a viable organization.

## Procedure

1. Define the system of interest and environment.
2. Identify primary operational units and their local autonomy.
3. Identify inter-unit conflicts/oscillations and coordination mechanisms.
4. Map current internal control/resource allocation.
5. Map environment/future sensing and adaptation.
6. Map identity/policy authority.
7. Check variety mismatch:
   - does control/observation have enough variety to respond to the environment?
   - where is information excessively amplified or attenuated?
8. Check recursion:
   - which subunits need their own local coordination/control/intelligence?
   - where is recursion falsely centralized?
9. Look for pathologies:
   - everything escalates upward;
   - duplicated policy;
   - operations starved by coordination overhead;
   - no future/environment sensing;
   - intelligence without authority path;
   - local autonomy without cohesion.
10. Compare the diagnosis against actual operating evidence before redesigning anything.

## Output

Produce:
- system/environment boundary;
- operational-unit map;
- coordination/control/intelligence/policy responsibilities;
- recursion map;
- variety bottlenecks;
- autonomy/cohesion tensions;
- candidate organizational experiments.

## Promotion law

Promote only responsibilities/boundaries supported by project evidence. Never create five mandatory services merely to mirror VSM terminology.

## Non-claims

- The five-function VSM mapping is a diagnostic model, not established universal empirical law.
- Requisite variety does not specify one software architecture.
- Autonomy is not absence of governance.
- Organizational viability is not product quality or business success.
- A neat VSM diagram does not prove the organization is viable.

## Stop condition

Stop when the observed coordination/adaptation bottleneck is explicit and can be tested with a smaller organizational change.
