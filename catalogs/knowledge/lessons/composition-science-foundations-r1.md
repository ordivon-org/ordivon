## Purpose

It separates external scientific foundations, Ordivon's operational synthesis, and hypotheses that remain unvalidated.

## Recombinant growth / innovation

Martin Weitzman's 1998 recombinant-growth model treats the production of new ideas as reconfiguration of old ideas and emphasizes that, as the idea pool grows, the number of possible combinations can become very large relative to the capacity to process them into usable innovation.

Adopted implication: primitive abundance can move the bottleneck toward search, selection and evaluation.

Not adopted as a universal claim: every useful innovation is recombination.

Source:
- https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120378-853d-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b

Agrawal, McHale and Oettl model innovation as finding useful combinations in complex knowledge spaces and study how better prediction about promising combinations can affect discovery.

Adopted implication: search quality over a combinatorial knowledge space can be economically important.

Source:
- https://www.nber.org/papers/w24541

## Recombinant uncertainty / technological search

Fleming's work on recombinant uncertainty emphasizes that unfamiliar components and new combinations can increase variance: exploration can produce both poor outcomes and breakthroughs.

Adopted implication: composition novelty is not automatically value; unfamiliar combinations require stronger evaluation.

Source:
- Lee Fleming, Management Science 47(1), 2001, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.47.1.117.10671

## Compositional verification

Assume-guarantee reasoning establishes a disciplined basis for verifying systems by reasoning about component assumptions and guarantees.

Adopted implication: valid composition requires assumption discharge, not merely interface fit.

Sources:
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20030017771
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20050185529
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220009652

## C-K design

C-K design distinguishes concept space from knowledge space and supports reasoning when the desired object itself is not yet fully known.

Adopted implication: open-ended innovation cannot always be represented as selecting among a fixed option set.

Source:
- https://www.tmci.minesparis.psl.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/1.2.pdf

## Systems, control and safety

Existing LEGO Theory already records Systems Engineering, DSM, feedback/state-space thinking and STPA foundations.

Adopted implication: components, relations, dynamics, environment and control constraints must remain distinguishable.

Source:

## Ordivon synthesis boundary

Ordivon may claim ownership of:

- an operational data model for composition-search tasks;
- routing among mature analytical methods;
- evidence-backed composition records;
- residual-build decision procedures;
- local tooling that makes those procedures executable.

Ordivon should not claim ownership of:

- recombinant innovation theory;
- systems engineering;
- C-K design;
- assume-guarantee verification;
- evolutionary search;
- control theory;
- other adopted external disciplines.

## Current hypotheses

The key R1 hypotheses remain falsifiable and unproven:

- mature ecosystems may produce large gains from relation/control changes without new primitives;
- discovery-before-build may reduce unnecessary ownership;
- explicit authority boundaries may improve substitutability;
- improved verification may expand safely usable composition space;
- agent-era implementation abundance may shift bottlenecks toward discovery/evaluation.

These should be tested prospectively rather than promoted through repetition in architecture prose.
