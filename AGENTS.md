# Ordivon Next working rules

These rules apply to work in this repository. Keep the common layer thinner than the external disciplines, standards, providers and domain owners it composes.

1. **Start from the real outcome and exact subject.** Bind the actual entity/work/artifact/system and the acceptance authority before inventing abstractions.
2. **External authority first.** Use current laws, contracts, standards, professional guidance, platform/provider rules and domain science as applicable. Ordivon mappings organize them; they do not replace them.
3. **Applicability is task-local.** Record why an authority/provider applies, is excluded, is deferred or is only advisory. More standards are not automatically better.
4. **Use the natural owner.** Business facts, workflow state, integration state, execution evidence, source bytes and domain acceptance stay with the mature system that naturally owns them. Do not create a universal Ordivon Task/Work database.
5. **Execution success is not semantic completion.** Verify consequential outcomes with domain-native validators/evidence and state the exact claim boundary.
6. **Dormant is not deficient.** Activate/install heavier providers only when a real workload demonstrates their distinct responsibility and substitution value.
7. **Preserve evidence history.** Append/supersede changed standing; do not rewrite historical evidence merely to match the present.
8. **Do not synthesize external authority.** Human experience, customer acceptance, legal authorization, certification, venue/platform decisions and other external assertions remain unresolved until the real authority supplies them.
9. **Scale management and controls with consequence.** A reversible local edit and an external production effect do not need the same process, review or authorization burden.
10. **For consequential multi-step, customer/business, multi-domain, quality/risk-sensitive or externally constrained work, activate the `enterprise-work` Skill.** For artifact production/verification, activate `artifact-work` as applicable.
