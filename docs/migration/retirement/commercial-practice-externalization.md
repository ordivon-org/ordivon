# Commercial practice: external-first operating boundaries

This is the current reference for commercial practice after the September 2026
R1–R3 migrations. It consolidates the three historical carriers; Git history
retains their exact acceptance snapshots. It is a method-selection reference,
not a separate commercial record system or a claim of customer acceptance.

## Use the natural owner of the fact

| Outcome | Established method or system | Evidence that matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer discovery and product fit | YC customer conversations, Lean Startup validated learning, Strategyzer business/value-proposition canvases | Actual buyer conversations, payment, retention and objections |
| Early sales and CRM | Founder-led sales, task-local pipeline, Frappe CRM when shared pipeline exists | Quotation, accepted/rejected deals and subsequent delivery |
| Transactional sales and accounting | ERPNext quotations, orders, invoices and ledger; bank/provider reconciliation | Submitted provider records and counterparties' payment |
| Pricing/packaging | Paid pilots, delivery economics and buyer objections; Stripe SaaS guidance only for SaaS products | Price, buyer response, cost, renewals and refunds |
| Customer feedback and complaints | Direct feedback, ISO 10004 / ISO 10002 if relevant | Actual customer outcomes and complaint resolution |
| Procurement and outsourcing | ERPNext supplier/RFQ/PO/invoice; ISO 20400 / ISO 37500 when warranted | Actual supplier terms, commitments and delivery |
| Recruitment/payroll | ISO 30405, applicable law and professional advice; Frappe HR when operationally useful | Actual employment terms, mandatory filings and pay receipts |
| Contracting | WorldCC contract management guidance; qualified counsel, approved SOW and terms | Bilateral commitment and accepted scope |
| Budget, cash and runway | ERPNext Budget/Budget Variance, GL, cash flow and a simple assumption-bound forecast | Reconciled cash, commitments, plan-versus-actual |
| Support/helpdesk | Direct support until coordination scale warrants Frappe Helpdesk; ISO/IEC 20000-1 only when applicable | Actual response, resolution, support burden and customer result |
| Company filing and quality | Current competent registration authorities, ISO 9001 if a QMS is needed | Official documents and independently accepted certification |

An ERP/CRM record is not customer demand. A budget is not cash. A ticket SLA is
not customer satisfaction. Professional standards guide a method but do not
replace law, actual delivery, or a competent external professional.

## Startup-stage activation

1. Use direct customer conversation and quotation before installing CRM or
   a company-specific sales methodology. An ERPNext CRM historical acceptance
   does not automatically qualify a newer ERPNext or Frappe CRM release.
2. Start pricing from a real scope, actual labor/nonlabor cost and observable
   buyer response. Do not create a private Pricing or Customer Success ontology.
3. Activate HR, procurement, legal review, ticketing and service-management
   software only for real staff, suppliers, contracts, complaints or support load.
4. Keep spending forecasts task-local until recurrent multi-scenario, multi-entity
   or departmental planning shows an operational need for a larger FP&A system.
5. Agents may perform bounded research, preparation, analysis and delegated
   operations; accountable human and professional owners remain responsible
   for legally binding commitments and their genuine-world acceptance.

**Upgrade rule:** prefer DELETE unnecessary bespoke methodology, then REUSE the
current external standard/provider, then COMPOSE. Admit an Ordivon-specific
commercial tool only when repeated observed consumer work demonstrates a gap.
