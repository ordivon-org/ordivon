# Commercial Strategy Current Facts R1

Date: 2026-09-19  
Status: CURRENT COMPANY-SPECIFIC WORKING FACTS / OPEN STRATEGIC CHOICES — not an Ordivon strategy framework.

## 0. Boundary

This document separates **company strategy** from the existing enterprise operating model.

- `ENTERPRISE_OPERATING_MODEL_R1` answers how bounded work is governed and delivered.
- Business Model Canvas / Value Proposition Canvas describe business-model and value-proposition hypotheses.
- Lean Startup structures validated learning under uncertainty.
- YC early-stage guidance supports founder-led customer discovery and sales.
- Roger Martin's Strategy Choice Cascade is used as the external strategy-choice structure: winning aspiration, where to play, how to win, required capabilities, and management systems.

None of those references proves market demand. Customer behavior, paid work, delivery economics, retention/repeat purchase and real outcomes remain the evidence.

## 1. Current established facts

The following claims are supported by current local source/evidence and should not be confused with future aspirations:

- Ordivon is still PRE-T0 as a legal company; no registered entity, corporate bank account, corporate payment rail or real company revenue is currently evidenced.
- The intended near-term commercialization motion is **service first, then productize repeated paid work** rather than building a speculative SaaS product before customer evidence.
- The technical substrate already spans research, engineering, artifacts, automation, security, distribution and other capabilities, but capability breadth is a supply-side fact, not a market strategy.
- Commercial operations deliberately use mature external methods/providers rather than private Ordivon Marketing/Channel/SEO/Attribution/Sales/GTM/Pricing/CRM/FP&A/HR/Support frameworks.
- Founder/accountable-owner exposure to buyer conversations, pricing objections, acceptance interpretation and product trade-offs is preserved because those are learning evidence.
- ERPNext/Frappe is proven only for the tested transactional/accounting slice; the acceptance fixtures were not real sales.
- No real ICP, beachhead vertical, beachhead workflow, paid pilot, repeat customer, product-market fit or proprietary learning advantage is yet proven.

## 2. Strategy Choice Cascade — current standing

| Choice | Current standing | Evidence / next proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winning aspiration | DIRECTIONAL: build a durable technical business that converts repeated high-value work into increasingly reusable software/automation | must be constrained by real customer economics rather than slogan-level ambition |
| Where to play | UNRESOLVED | select an initial customer segment + concrete high-value workflow from customer discovery and paid experiments |
| How to win | HYPOTHESIS: occupy a privileged execution/evidence/evaluation position inside a valuable workflow, while using mature commodity components underneath | prove through customer preference, acceptance, measurable outcome improvement, repeat purchase and defensible learning rights |
| Capabilities required | PARTLY ESTABLISHED technically; commercially unproven | bind only the capabilities required by the selected workflow; retire unused capability-as-strategy claims |

Strategy is therefore **not complete** until the unresolved Where-to-Play / How-to-Win choices receive market evidence.

## 3. Beachhead workflow selection — company-specific decision criteria

Do not create a universal Ordivon scoring ontology. For the next commercial search, compare candidate workflows using explicit task-local evidence around:

- economic consequence: solving the workflow is worth enough money to the buyer;
- recurrence: the workflow happens repeatedly or generates adjacent follow-on work;
- evaluability: success/failure can be judged by an external/customer/domain authority;
- access: a small new supplier can realistically enter the workflow;
- time-to-value and sales friction: evidence can be obtained without a multi-year procurement cycle;
- delivery controllability: Ordivon can commit to bounded outputs rather than uncontrollable third-party outcomes;
- learning visibility: execution traces, corrections and downstream outcomes can be observed where contract/privacy rules permit;
- lawful learning rights: customer contracts can clearly define what may be retained, aggregated, benchmarked or used for evaluation/product improvement;
- productization potential: repeated engagements reuse a material portion of task structure, integration, verification or domain logic;
- substitution pressure: commodity model/API/tool improvements do not erase the entire customer relationship or workflow position.

These are current Ordivon decision requirements, not a general-purpose strategy method.

## 4. First-offer rule

Do not launch a broad promise such as "AI transformation" or "anything Ordivon can do."

The first commercial offer should bind:

```text
specific buyer
+ specific recurring workflow/problem
+ bounded deliverable
+ explicit inputs/access
+ acceptance criteria
+ price/payment
+ change boundary
+ security/privacy/IP terms
+ measurable before/after or outcome evidence where practical
```

A capability package is not itself an offer.

## 5. Proprietary learning loop — current hypothesis

The strategic hypothesis is not "own as much customer data as possible." The desired compounding position is:

```text
customer context
  -> bounded task
  -> plan / tool / model choices
  -> actions
  -> evidence
  -> customer/domain acceptance or correction
  -> downstream outcome
  -> reusable evaluation / operational learning where authorized
```

The scarce asset is a **legitimate, recurring position in the workflow plus permission to learn from the resulting evidence**.

No customer data, trace or outcome may be treated as training/evaluation/product-improvement material merely because Ordivon technically handled it.

## 6. Learning-rights contract boundary

Before any customer-derived information is reused beyond direct service delivery, the applicable agreement/privacy/security terms must distinguish at least:

- customer content and confidential information;
- service-operation logs and security/audit records;
- task/execution traces;
- human corrections and acceptance/rejection signals;
- downstream outcome evidence;
- de-identified/aggregated operational metrics;
- benchmark/evaluation use;
- model training or fine-tuning use;
- product-improvement use;
- retention period and deletion obligations;
- subprocessors/model providers and data-transfer constraints;
- ownership/licence of customer-specific deliverables and reusable pre-existing/general know-how.

Actual legal wording and jurisdictional obligations remain with applicable law and qualified counsel. "Customer allowed the service" does not imply "customer allowed training."

## 7. Service -> product promotion boundary

Do not promote a workflow into a product because it was technically automatable once.

A product candidate becomes worth explicit evaluation only after paid work shows material repetition across engagements/customers in:

- buyer job/problem;
- required inputs/integrations;
- execution structure;
- verification/acceptance criteria;
- recurring operational burden;
- willingness to pay;
- reusable implementation/evaluation assets.

No fixed numeric threshold is frozen before the first paid engagements. The evidence set should make it possible to show that software reuse can reduce marginal delivery cost or improve speed/quality without destroying the service's value.

## 8A. Active discovery surface — university students

As of 2026-09-20, university students are admitted only as a **high-priority discovery population**, not a validated ICP.

Current task-local census and experiment plans:

- `STUDENT_MARKET_PRIMITIVE_CENSUS_R1.md` — initial global/high-agency decomposition;
- `STUDENT_BEACHHEAD_DISCOVERY_EXPERIMENTS_R1.md`;
- `CHINA_UNIVERSITY_STUDENT_WORKFLOW_CENSUS_R2.md` — mainland-China-specific campus/information/deadline/transition workflows;
- `CHINA_STUDENT_MARKET_DISCOVERY_PLAN_R2.md`;
- `CHINA_STUDENT_SEED_PORTFOLIO_R3.md` — seed-level decomposition of opportunity/eligibility/deadline/evidence/action loops;
- `CHINA_STUDENT_SEED_EXPERIMENTS_R3.md` — concierge experiments before product build.

R2 broadens the discovery surface beyond high-agency students. Current hypotheses now have two market layers:

1. **mass student utility** — opportunity intelligence, campus obligations, second-classroom/competition/internship information, postgraduate/recommendation/public-recruitment/study-abroad navigation;
2. **high-agency premium workflows** — research execution/reproducibility, technical projects/GitHub/competition execution, evidence-backed career transition, student-founder execution.

Generic tutoring, generic coding assistance, literature search, résumé generation and generic job search remain intentionally outside the beachhead because mature providers already occupy those layers.

A new China-specific strategic hypothesis is that the broad residual value may be:
`verified information + personal eligibility + deadline/prerequisite graph + next action + evidence/outcome`,
with AI hidden as an implementation primitive rather than sold as a chatbot.

No student ICP, payer or beachhead is validated until interviews, commitment-quality evidence and paid pilots exist.

The next commercial work should therefore be market-facing rather than infrastructure-facing:

