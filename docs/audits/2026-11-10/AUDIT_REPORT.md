## Formal/management review of selected change

Source-subtraction refinement obligation: if removed historical component H has no present production caller in audited source and installed entrypoints, and its bytes remain recoverable in Git, deleting H must leave all currently asserted public/native producer contracts unaffected. This is a bounded conditional claim. It does NOT universally prove no external dynamic caller. For unpromoted R2 mailbox, independent reviewer seq25909 specifically corrected false equivalence to rclone temporary credential/presign capabilities. The correct business problem is dead custom responsibility, not replacing a current consumer without a drop-in feature.

A 5002-row ledger, 21 owner names or a PASS on repo guards is not sufficient to claim whole-monorepo modernization complete. Phase 4 may safely execute only the bounded audited subset while the owner-specific high-priority architectural reviews remain delegated.
