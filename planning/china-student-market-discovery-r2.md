# China Student Market Discovery Plan R2

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: PRE-EVIDENCE FIELD PLAN

## 0. Goal

Test whether the China-specific student opportunity/obligation layer is a stronger initial market surface than a pure high-agency-student product.

Do not build a product first.

## 1. Discovery cells

### Cell 1 — ordinary undergraduates
Look for:
- missed deadlines;
- course attendance friction;
- campus run;
- second-classroom credits;
- notices scattered across groups/apps;
- how they currently remember obligations.

### Cell 2 — competition / scholarship / activity seekers
Look for:
- how they discover competitions;
- how late they discover them;
- how they judge "worth doing";
- school/internal registration steps;
- advisor/team/material bottlenecks;
- mapping to second-classroom/awards/推免.

### Cell 3 — 考研 / 推免 students
Look for:
- which rules are difficult to interpret;
- where programme/school information is scattered;
- what private spreadsheets/groups/services they use;
- deadline/material failures;
- willingness to pay for trusted personalized navigation.

### Cell 4 — internship / employment / 考公 / 考编 students
Look for:
- official vs social information sources;
- eligibility screening burden;
- duplicate applications;
- tracking;
- false/outdated job information;
- document/deadline burden.

### Cell 5 — study-abroad students
Look for:
- information source hierarchy;
- agency use;
- programme comparison;
- scholarship/cost uncertainty;
- document workflow;
- trust/conflict problems;
- what they would pay a neutral tool for.

### Cell 6 — research / project / GitHub students
Retain R1 evidence collection:
- long-chain execution;
- reproducibility;
- project proof;
- competition/open-source/career linkage.

## 2. New interview questions

In addition to the R1 problem interview:

- "过去一个月你错过过什么通知、活动、报名或截止日期？"
- "你平时从哪里知道比赛/实习/推免/二课/学校通知？"
- "哪些群你不敢退？为什么？"
- "学校里哪些 App/公众号/群你必须每天看？"
- "有没有你到很晚才知道、知道后已经来不及准备的机会？"
- "你怎么判断一条信息是真的、最新的？"
- "哪类规则你每次都要去问学长学姐？"
- "你有没有自己维护 Excel/备忘录/收藏夹/群置顶？"
- "如果一个工具每天只告诉你与你相关的 3 件事，你会不会长期用？"
- "如果它告诉你‘为什么与你相关’并附官方原文，你是否更信任？"
- "你愿意为不漏掉重要机会付多少钱？一次性还是订阅？"
- "如果免费版够用，什么功能会让你付费？"

## 3. Prototype without product

First prototype may be a manually/agentically operated concierge:

Input:
- university;
- major;
- year;
- goals (competition / internship / 推免 / 考研 / study abroad / public recruitment etc.);
- optional current activities/credits.

Output once or twice per week:
- relevant verified opportunities/obligations;
- why relevant;
- authoritative source;
- deadline;
- prerequisites;
- next action;
- unknowns requiring school/authority confirmation.

Measure:
- opened;
- acted;
- saved;
- shared;
- applied/registered;
- missed;
- false-positive;
- false-negative discovered by student;
- willingness to continue/pay.

This can be delivered manually before any app exists.

## 4. Four prototype offer probes

### Probe A — "本周与你有关"
A personalized verified weekly digest of opportunities/deadlines.

### Probe B — "大学四年机会地图"
A semester-level map:
competition / scholarship / research / internship / second-classroom / exams / graduate-school milestones.

### Probe C — "保研/考研/留学行动图"
Source-backed eligibility + timeline + document graph for one chosen route.

### Probe D — "校园事务防漏"
Course/attendance/PE/second-classroom/admin deadline reminder and state tracker.

## 5. Kill conditions

Kill/narrow a surface if:
- students already solve it reliably with one existing app;
- opportunity information cannot be legally/technically collected with acceptable freshness;
- personalization requires more data than the value justifies;
- students ignore alerts and rely on peer groups anyway;
- false-positive/false-negative cost destroys trust;
- no payer can be found and consumer willingness to pay is effectively zero;
- institutional integration is mandatory before any value is delivered.

## 6. Product posture if evidence is positive

Do not lead with AI.

Lead with:
- "别错过";
- "这件事与你有关";
- "规则来自官方";
- "下一步做什么";
- "截止前完成";
- "你的材料还缺什么".

AI stays behind the interface for extraction, matching, explanation and workflow assistance.
